CLAUSE 01 — SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY
[REF: ASME B31.8 Section 800 / Para. 800.1]

ASME B31.8 covers the design, fabrication, installation, inspection, testing,
and safety of gas transmission and distribution piping systems (خطوط أنابيب نقل
وتوزيع الغاز). The standard applies onshore gas transmission lines, gathering
lines (خطوط التجميع), distribution mains (خطوط التوزيع الرئيسية), and service
lines from the wellhead or production facility to the customer meter. Offshore
gas pipelines that transition onshore within the first valve assembly are also
covered. This standard does NOT apply to: piping within compressor stations
(beyond the station block valve), gas-fired appliances downstream of the
customer meter, or liquid petroleum pipelines governed by B31.4. Inspectors
must confirm which B31 code governs the system before applying criteria.
ASME B31.8 is frequently referenced alongside DOT Part 192 (USA) and EGPC/EGAS
regulations in Egypt.

ACCEPTANCE:
- System is a gas transmission, gathering, or distribution line per Para. 800.1.
- All design, fabrication, and testing documentation references ASME B31.8-2022
  or the edition specified in the purchase order / regulatory authority.
- Code edition year on engineering documentation matches physical nameplate and
  inspection records.

REJECTION:
- Application of B31.8 to liquid petroleum pipelines (use B31.4) — reject code
  reference immediately and halt inspection until correct standard is identified.
- Absence of any code reference on engineering drawings for new construction —
  reject; require code identification before pressure testing.
- System operating above MOP with no B31.8-compliant design documentation —
  immediate flagging for engineering review.

CLAUSE 02 — LOCATION CLASS DEFINITIONS
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 840.2, Table 840.3.1]

Location class (تصنيف الموقع) is determined by counting the number of buildings
intended for human occupancy within a corridor 1 mile (1.6 km) long and 220 yards
(200 m) wide on each side of the pipeline centerline — a "unit area" of 1 mile.
Class 1 applies where 10 or fewer buildings for human occupancy exist; Class 2
covers areas with 11 to 45 such buildings; Class 3 applies to areas with 46 or
more buildings or in any area within 100 yards of a building that is occupied by
20 or more persons at any one time; Class 4 applies where multi-story buildings
are prevalent and where traffic is heavy or where there may be numerous pedestrians.
Location class governs design factor F, inspection frequency, patrolling schedule,
test pressure, and leak survey requirements — it is the single most important
classification an inspector (مفتش الخط) must verify in the field. Class can
upgrade (increase) as population grows; downgrade requires regulatory approval.

ACCEPTANCE:
- Class 1: ≤10 buildings/mile corridor. Two subdivisions: Div 1 and Div 2.
- Class 2: 11–45 buildings/mile corridor.
- Class 3: ≥46 buildings/mile OR within 100 yd of a structure occupied by
  ≥20 persons at any one time.
- Class 4: Multi-story buildings prevalent (≥4 stories), heavy traffic.
- Location class documented in pipeline design dossier and reviewed annually.

REJECTION:
- Location class not re-evaluated after community development (urban encroachment
  — الزحف العمراني) within 1-mile corridor — reject; require immediate reassessment.
- Pipeline operating with Class 1 design factor in what is now a Class 3 or
  Class 4 area without MAOP reduction or pressure test upgrade — REJECT; pipeline
  must be de-rated or upgraded within regulatory timeline.
- Absence of a population density survey in the integrity management file — reject
  IMP documentation as incomplete.

CLAUSE 03 — DESIGN PRESSURE FORMULA
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 841.11 / Equation 841.114(a)]

The design pressure formula for steel pipe is:
    P = (2 × S × t / D) × F × E × T
where:
  P = maximum allowable operating pressure, MAOP (أقصى ضغط تشغيل مسموح), in psi
  S = specified minimum yield strength, SMYS (أدنى قيم مقاومة الخضوع المحددة),
      in psi per applicable API or ASTM pipe standard
  t = nominal wall thickness (سماكة الجدار الإسمية), in inches
  D = nominal outside diameter (القطر الخارجي الإسمي), in inches
  F = design factor (عامل التصميم) — see Clause 04
  E = longitudinal joint factor (عامل الوصل الطولي) — see Clause 06
  T = temperature derating factor (عامل التخفيض الحراري) — see below.
T = 1.000 for temperatures at or below 250°F (121°C); T = 0.967 at 300°F;
T = 0.933 at 350°F; T = 0.900 at 400°F; T = 0.867 at 450°F (ASME B31.8
Table 841.116A). At operating temperatures ≤250°F (the vast majority of gas
pipelines), T = 1.000 and drops out of the calculation. Inspectors must verify
all five inputs against the actual pipe mill certificate (شهادة المصنع) and
design drawing before accepting any MAOP stamp.

ACCEPTANCE:
- All five formula inputs confirmed from traceable, certified documents.
- Calculated MAOP equals or exceeds actual operating pressure by ≥0 psi (no
  deficit permitted — actual OP must be ≤ calculated MAOP at all times).
- T factor = 1.000 for gas temperatures ≤250°F (121°C) — confirm gas temperature
  from SCADA or process data.
- Formula applied using nominal wall thickness minus no mill tolerance deduction
  unless specifically required by design; B31.8 Para. 841.11 uses nominal t.

REJECTION:
- Any formula input (S, t, D, F, E, T) taken from an unverified or assumed value
  with no mill certificate or drawing backing — REJECT design basis.
- Calculated MAOP less than actual operating pressure — REJECT; immediate
  shutdown or pressure reduction required until engineering review complete.
- Temperature derating factor not applied when gas temperature >250°F — REJECT
  calculation; pipeline operating above permitted MAOP.
- SMYS value used exceeding the API 5L grade listed on mill certificate — REJECT.

CLAUSE 04 — DESIGN FACTORS (F) PER LOCATION CLASS
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 841.114 / Table 841.114A]

The design factor F is the single largest variable controlling MAOP in a gas
transmission pipeline (خط نقل الغاز). B31.8 Table 841.114A assigns F as follows:
  - Class 1, Division 1 (bare or minimal coated, no cathodic protection
    in remote areas with ≤10 buildings): F = 0.80
  - Class 1, Division 2 (Class 1 with cathodic protection and coated pipe): F = 0.72
  - Class 2: F = 0.60
  - Class 3: F = 0.50
  - Class 4: F = 0.40
Note: Class 1 Div 1 at 0.80 is permitted ONLY for cross-country pipelines with
established cathodic protection — field inspectors (مفتشو الحقل) must confirm CP
system is active before accepting F = 0.80. Division 1 vs Division 2 distinction
within Class 1 relates to the presence or absence of cathodic protection and
coating, not just building count. A pipeline originally designed as Class 1 Div 1
in an area that becomes Class 2 must have its F factor reduced from 0.80 to 0.60,
which directly reduces allowable MAOP and may require physical pressure reduction
or line upgrade.

ACCEPTANCE:
- F = 0.80: Class 1 Div 1 only — confirm CP system active, coating intact.
- F = 0.72: Class 1 Div 2 — confirm CP + coating, area remains Class 1.
- F = 0.60: Class 2 — verify population count 11–45 buildings/mile.
- F = 0.50: Class 3 — verify population count ≥46 buildings/mile OR proximity
  criteria met.
- F = 0.40: Class 4 — multi-story, high density confirmed.
- Design factor documented on P&ID and pipeline design dossier with location
  class justification.

REJECTION:
- F = 0.80 applied without active cathodic protection (حماية كاثودية) system —
  REJECT; immediately reduce to F = 0.72 maximum or lower per actual class.
- Design factor not revised after location class upgrade due to urban growth —
  REJECT; pipeline operating above recalculated MAOP.
- F value higher than permitted for assigned class — REJECT; engineering de-rating
  required before continued operation.
- Use of F > 0.80 for any steel gas transmission line — absolute REJECTION; no
  B31.8 provision allows F > 0.80 for gas transmission.

CLAUSE 05 — WALL THICKNESS SELECTION AND SMYS REQUIREMENTS
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 841.11 / 841.12 / API 5L Table 1]

Pipe for gas transmission and distribution shall be manufactured in accordance
with API 5L, ASTM A53, ASTM A106, or other standards listed in B31.8 Table
841.111. The minimum wall thickness (الحد الأدنى لسماكة الجدار) used in design
must be the nominal mill wall thickness as certified; mill tolerance is not
deducted by B31.8 unless the designer specifically applies it. For distribution
piping, minimum wall thickness for steel pipe NPS 2 and smaller shall be
Schedule 40 or equivalent per Para. 841.12. SMYS values by grade:
  - API 5L Grade B: SMYS = 35,000 psi (241 MPa)
  - API 5L X42: SMYS = 42,000 psi (290 MPa)
  - API 5L X52: SMYS = 52,000 psi (359 MPa)
  - API 5L X60: SMYS = 60,000 psi (414 MPa)
  - API 5L X65: SMYS = 65,000 psi (448 MPa)
  - API 5L X70: SMYS = 70,000 psi (483 MPa)
  - API 5L X80: SMYS = 80,000 psi (552 MPa)
Inspectors must verify pipe grade from the stencil/marking on the pipe body and
cross-check against the mill test certificate (MTR — شهادة اختبار المصنع) before
allowing installation. Substitution of a lower-grade pipe for a higher-grade
design is not permitted without engineering recalculation of MAOP.

ACCEPTANCE:
- Pipe grade and heat number match the approved MTO (material take-off) and MTR.
- Wall thickness measured by UT gauge within -0% to +mill tolerance of nominal
  (B31.8 does not deduct mill tolerance from design calculation by default).
- SMYS on MTR ≥ specified grade SMYS (e.g., X52 MTR shows ≥52,000 psi yield).
- CVN (Charpy V-notch — اختبار شاربي) toughness requirements met per API 5L for
  high-pressure sour service where H₂S is present (NACE MR0175 cross-reference).

REJECTION:
- Pipe grade marking absent, illegible, or inconsistent with MTR — REJECT pipe;
  do not install until traceability is restored.
- Measured wall thickness below nominal by more than mill tolerance
  (API 5L: -12.5% for seamless, -0% for ERW per relevant table) — REJECT pipe.
- MTR not available for pressure-containing pipe — REJECT; no substitution or
  engineering assumption permitted.
- SMYS on MTR below specified grade minimum — REJECT; pipe cannot be used for
  intended design MAOP.

CLAUSE 06 — LONGITUDINAL WELD JOINT FACTOR (E)
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 841.115 / Table 841.115A]

The weld joint factor E accounts for the efficiency (كفاءة الوصل الطولي) of the
longitudinal seam weld in the pipe body — not the field girth welds. E values
per B31.8 Table 841.115A:
  - Seamless pipe (أنبوب بدون درز): E = 1.000
  - Electric Resistance Welded (ERW — لحام المقاومة الكهربائية): E = 1.000
    (post-1970 ERW meeting API 5L SR4/SR15 — full seam NDE required)
  - Electric Flash Welded (EFW): E = 1.000
  - Submerged Arc Welded (SAW — لحام القوس المغمور) — double seam, full RT:
    E = 1.000
  - Submerged Arc Welded — single seam: E = 1.000 (with full RT)
  - Electric Fusion Welded (EFW) — single pass, partial RT: E = 0.800
  - Furnace Butt Welded (FBW — لحام الفرن): E = 0.600
  - Spiral (helical) welded, SAW, full RT: E = 1.000
  - Pre-1970 ERW pipe (low-frequency ERW): E = 0.800 — this is a critical field
    issue; older vintage pipe must be identified and derated accordingly.
Inspectors must check the pipe specification and purchase date; unidentified
vintage ERW on older gathering lines may be operating with E = 1.000 applied
incorrectly, resulting in an overstated MAOP.

ACCEPTANCE:
- E = 1.000: Seamless, post-1970 HF-ERW (API 5L), SAW (full RT), EFW (full RT).
- E = 0.800: Pre-1970 LF-ERW, single-pass EFW with partial RT.
- E = 0.600: Furnace butt welded — essentially legacy pipe; rarely new construction.
- Pipe manufacture year and seam type documented in pipeline records.
- Full seam NDE documentation available for any pipe using E = 1.000 on a
  welded seam type.

REJECTION:
- E = 1.000 applied to pre-1970 LF-ERW pipe — REJECT calculation; derate to
  E = 0.800 immediately, recalculate MAOP, reduce pressure if required.
- E = 1.000 applied to furnace butt welded pipe — REJECT; E = 0.600 mandatory.
- Pipe seam type unknown (no records, illegible markings) and E = 1.000 assumed
  — REJECT; use E = 0.800 as conservative minimum until seam type confirmed.
- No seam NDE records for welded pipe claiming E = 1.000 — REJECT; require full
  seam RT/UT records or apply lower E factor.

CLAUSE 07 — WELDING PROCEDURE AND WELDER QUALIFICATION
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 823 / 826 — Reference API 1104 (Ed. 22)]

All welding on gas pipeline systems covered by B31.8 shall be performed in
accordance with a qualified welding procedure specification (WPS — مواصفات إجراء
اللحام) and by welders (لحامون) or welding operators qualified per API Standard
1104, "Welding of Pipelines and Related Facilities." The WPS must be qualified
by a pre-production procedure qualification record (PQR — سجل تأهيل الإجراء)
using destructive and non-destructive tests as required by API 1104 Section 5.
Each welder must pass a performance qualification test (PQT) specific to the
process, position, pipe diameter, and wall thickness range to be welded in
production. Welder qualification expires after 6 months of non-use of the
specific process; continuity of qualification requires work records showing
production welds within the preceding 6-month period. All WPS, PQR, and welder
qualification records (سجلات التأهيل) must be maintained by the contractor and
available for inspector review at the job site.

ACCEPTANCE:
- WPS is pre-qualified or procedure-tested per API 1104 Section 5.
- PQR test results on file: tensile, nick-break, bend tests passing API 1104 Table 3.
- Each production welder has a current PQT (within 6-month continuity window)
  covering the position, diameter, and wall thickness being welded.
- Welder identification (الرقم التعريفي للحام) stamped or recorded on joint record
  for every production weld — mandatory for traceability.
- Preheat temperature per WPS confirmed by contact thermometer or Tempilstik
  before arc start — API 1104 / B31.8 Para. 823.

REJECTION:
- Welder performing production work with no current PQT on file — STOP WORK;
  weld is unacceptable; cut out and re-weld with qualified welder.
- WPS applied outside its qualified range (e.g., wall thickness, pipe grade,
  preheat, filler metal) — REJECT weld; evaluate for repair or removal.
- No preheat applied when WPS requires minimum preheat — REJECT weld;
  evaluate by NDE, likely require cut-out.
- Welder identification not recorded — REJECT weld record; weld remains but
  full NDE (100% RT or UT) mandatory before acceptance.
- WPS not signed and approved by a responsible welding engineer — REJECT WPS.

CLAUSE 08 — NONDESTRUCTIVE EXAMINATION (NDE) REQUIREMENTS
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 826 / Table 826.1]

B31.8 requires NDE of girth welds (اللحامات الدائرية) based on location class —
higher classes demand more intensive examination. Inspection methods used are
radiographic testing (RT — الفحص الإشعاعي), ultrasonic testing (UT — الفحص
بالموجات فوق الصوتية), magnetic particle testing (MT — الفحص بالجسيمات
المغناطيسية), and liquid penetrant testing (PT — فحص اختراق السوائل). Per
B31.8 Table 826.1 (minimum NDE requirements for steel pipe welds):
  - Class 1: NDE of girth welds — 10% random RT or equivalent (مضغوط عشوائي
    بنسبة 10%)
  - Class 2: NDE — 15% random RT or equivalent
  - Class 3: NDE — 100% RT or equivalent for all tie-in welds; 15% random for
    others unless crossings apply
  - Class 4: NDE — 100% RT or equivalent for ALL girth welds
  - All pipeline crossings (highway, railroad, river — معابر الطرق والسكك
    الحديدية والأنهار): 100% RT regardless of class
  - All compressor station welds: 100% RT
  - Repair welds: 100% RT on the repaired joint regardless of class
Automated Ultrasonic Testing (AUT) is accepted as an equivalent to RT where
validated per B31.8 Appendix B and API 1104 Annex A.

ACCEPTANCE:
- Class 1: ≥10% of girth welds examined per lot; defects found trigger additional
  testing per API 1104 Section 9 (if defect rate >0% in sample, expand to 20%
  then 100%).
- Class 2: ≥15% examined per lot; same defect-triggered escalation applies.
- Class 3 tie-in and Class 4: 100% NDE with no welds skipped.
- All crossing welds: 100% NDE — no exception.
- AUT procedures validated and demonstrated equivalent sensitivity to RT.
- NDE performed by Level II or Level III technician (فني) per SNT-TC-1A or
  ISO 9712, qualified for the method and pipe size.

REJECTION:
- Class 4 or crossing welds with any untested girth welds — REJECT; NDE must be
  completed before backfilling (الردم).
- NDE performed by uncertified or expired-certification technician — REJECT results;
  repeat NDE by qualified personnel.
- RT image quality inadequate (IQI sensitivity not met) — REJECT radiograph; repeat.
- Defect rate in Class 1 or 2 random sample >0% without expanding to additional
  welds per API 1104 — REJECT NDE program; escalate testing immediately.
- Backfilling over un-NDE'd welds in Class 3 or 4 — STOP WORK; excavate and
  perform NDE before re-covering.

CLAUSE 09 — RT AND UT WELD ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 826 — API 1104 Sections 9 and 11 (incorporated by reference)]

B31.8 Para. 826 invokes API 1104 for weld acceptance criteria. API 1104 Section 9
(radiography) and Section 11 (UT) govern acceptance of girth weld flaws. Key
radiographic acceptance criteria (معايير القبول الإشعاعي):
  - Incomplete fusion (انصهار ناقص) and incomplete penetration: NOT ACCEPTABLE
    in any length — reject if visible on film.
  - Burn-through: NOT ACCEPTABLE if ≥3 mm (1/8 in) in any dimension.
  - Porosity (مسامية): individual pore ≤3.2 mm (1/8 in) acceptable; cluster
    porosity ≤ 6.4 mm (1/4 in) in total aggregate length per any 12-inch
    weld length; elongated porosity ≤ 50.8 mm (2 in) in any 12-inch length.
  - Slag inclusions (شوائب الخبث): individual slag ≤ 50.8 mm (2 in) length OR
    ≤ 1/3 weld seam length, whichever is less; width ≤ 3.2 mm (1/8 in).
  - Cracks (شقوق): NOT ACCEPTABLE in any length — immediate rejection.
  - Undercutting (الحفر الجانبي): depth ≤ 0.8 mm (1/32 in) and length ≤ 50.8 mm
    (2 in) in any 12-inch length.
  - Internal concavity (التقعر الداخلي): acceptable if RT density shows wall
    thickness not reduced below minimum; otherwise reject.
All rejectable indications must be repaired per API 1104 Section 10 (repair) or
the joint must be cut out and re-welded.

ACCEPTANCE:
- Porosity: single pore ≤3.2 mm; cluster total ≤6.4 mm per 12 in; elongated
  ≤50.8 mm per 12 in.
- Slag: individual ≤50.8 mm long AND ≤3.2 mm wide; aggregate ≤ 1/3 seam length.
- Undercut: depth ≤0.8 mm AND length ≤50.8 mm per 12 in.
- No cracks. No incomplete fusion. No burn-through ≥3 mm.
- IQI (image quality indicator — مؤشر جودة الصورة): 2% wire or hole IQI visible
  on film as required by API 1104.

REJECTION:
- Any crack detected — REJECT WELD; cut-out or repair (crack removal to sound
  metal + full re-weld + 100% RT of repair).
- Incomplete fusion or incomplete penetration — REJECT WELD; repair mandatory.
- Porosity exceeding limits — REJECT; repair affected area, re-RT.
- Slag exceeding limits — REJECT; repair, re-RT.
- Undercut depth >0.8 mm — REJECT; grind and verify remaining wall ≥ minimum;
  may require weld build-up.
- IQI not visible on film — REJECT radiograph; retake with proper technique.

CLAUSE 10 — HYDROSTATIC PRESSURE TEST — NEW CONSTRUCTION
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 841.3 / 847.1 / Table 841.3.2]

All new gas transmission pipelines (خطوط نقل الغاز الجديدة) must be hydrostatically
tested (اختبار ضغط مائي) with water before being placed in service unless a
pneumatic test is specifically authorized. The minimum test pressure (ضغط الاختبار
الأدنى) shall be:
  - Class 1 and Class 2 pipelines: 1.25 × MOP (maximum operating pressure)
    sustained for a minimum of 8 continuous hours.
  - Class 3 and Class 4 pipelines: 1.50 × MOP sustained for a minimum of
    8 continuous hours.
The test pressure shall not exceed 100% of the SMYS-based yield pressure
(i.e., shall not cause permanent deformation of the pipe wall). Test medium
must be water; if dewatering is not feasible for short-duration testing in remote
areas, a variance under Para. 841.3 may allow pneumatic test subject to Class 1
or Class 2 restriction — see Clause 12. After a successful hydrostatic test, the
test establishes the pipeline's ability to operate at MOP; the MAOP is then set
at or below the pressure that is 80% of the test pressure (for Class 1 Div 1).

ACCEPTANCE:
- Class 1 & 2: test pressure ≥ 1.25 × MOP, held for ≥8 hours, no pressure drop
  >1% not explained by temperature correction.
- Class 3 & 4: test pressure ≥ 1.50 × MOP, held for ≥8 hours, same drop criterion.
- Test pressure ≤ yield pressure (100% SMYS yield at test temperature) —
  calculated from: P_yield = 2 × SMYS × t / D.
- Chart recorder traces (مسجل الضغط والحرارة) maintained continuously for
  full hold duration.
- Test manifold calibrated pressure gauges (2 minimum), calibration traceable
  to national standard, calibration date within 1 year.

REJECTION:
- Test pressure below the required multiple of MOP — REJECT test; re-pressurize
  to correct level and restart the 8-hour hold.
- Unaccounted pressure drop (تسرب) during hold period — REJECT test; locate and
  repair leak; repeat full test.
- Hold time less than 8 hours — REJECT test; extend hold time and restart count.
- Test pressure exceeding yield pressure — REJECT; risk of permanent deformation
  (over-strain); evaluate pipe condition before re-test.
- Chart recorder absent or malfunctioning during test — REJECT test records;
  repeat test with functioning recording equipment.
- Water not fully evacuated (dewatered) after test before gas introduction —
  REJECT commissioning; dewater to <0.1 L/m of pipe before gas in.

CLAUSE 11 — HYDROSTATIC TEST HOLD TIME AND TEMPERATURE REQUIREMENTS
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 847.1(d) / 841.3.2]

The hydrostatic test hold period (فترة التثبيت) must be uninterrupted for the
minimum duration specified. During the hold, pressure and temperature must be
continuously monitored and recorded at a minimum at both the high and low
elevation test segments. Temperature correction (تصحيح درجة الحرارة) is
mandatory: a pressure drop caused solely by a decrease in water temperature does
not constitute a test failure provided the corrected pressure-temperature
relationship remains stable. The temperature-corrected acceptance criterion is:
if ambient temperature drops by ΔT°F, the resulting pressure change ΔP = 0.013 ×
P_test × ΔT (°F) is acceptable as a temperature effect — any additional drop
beyond this correction is a LEAK (تسرب). Test water must be free of sediment and
contaminants that could damage the pipeline interior (especially critical for
gas distribution PE pipe or internally coated transmission lines). Freeze risk:
if ambient temperature is forecast to drop below 35°F (2°C) during or within
12 hours of the test, inhibited water or other antifreeze measures are required.

ACCEPTANCE:
- Hold period: ≥8 hours continuous per Class requirement (Clause 10).
- Pressure drop: ≤1% of initial test pressure per 8 hours, after temperature
  correction — or zero unexplained drop.
- Temperature recorded at intervals ≤15 minutes throughout hold period.
- Elevation difference accounted for: test pressure at high point ≥ 1.25 × MOP
  (or 1.50 × MOP for Class 3-4) at that elevation.
- Test records signed by certified test supervisor and inspector.

REJECTION:
- Hold period interrupted by pressure fluctuation requiring re-pressurization
  above test pressure — REJECT; reset hold timer from zero after re-pressurization.
- Unaccounted pressure drop >1% per 8-hour period — REJECT; classify as leak;
  locate, repair, retest.
- Temperature not recorded — REJECT test data; temperature correction cannot
  be applied; retest with monitoring.
- No elevation correction applied for pipeline with ≥30 ft (9 m) elevation
  change over test section — REJECT test acceptance; recalculate pressures
  at all critical elevations.

CLAUSE 12 — PNEUMATIC PRESSURE TEST
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 847.3 / 841.3.4]

Pneumatic testing (الاختبار الهوائي أو الغازي) with gas (inert gas, air, or the
operating gas) is permitted ONLY when: (a) the line cannot be filled with water
due to structural or operational reasons documented in writing; OR (b) the pipe
material is not compatible with water (e.g., certain PE distribution lines where
water ingress cannot be tolerated). For steel gas transmission pipelines,
pneumatic testing is permissible for Class 1 and Class 2 locations ONLY.
Pneumatic test pressure is LIMITED to 1.10 × MOP (110% of maximum operating
pressure). The pneumatic test is inherently more hazardous than hydrostatic
testing because compressed gas stores far more energy than liquid water at the
same pressure (فارق الطاقة المخزنة). Special safety requirements apply:
  - All personnel must be evacuated from the hazard zone (منطقة الخطر) — 200 m
    minimum radius from the test section during pressurization above 80% of
    test pressure.
  - Pressure must be raised in steps: 25%, 50%, 75%, 90%, 100%, with 10-minute
    holds at each step for visual/audio inspection.
  - Test pressure hold: minimum 4 hours at 1.10 × MOP.

ACCEPTANCE:
- Pneumatic test authorized in writing by the responsible engineer with documented
  justification for inability to hydrotest.
- Test pressure: ≤ 1.10 × MOP. No exceedance permitted.
- Location: Class 1 or Class 2 ONLY — no pneumatic testing in Class 3 or Class 4.
- Step pressurization performed with holds at 25%, 50%, 75%, 90%, 100% of
  test pressure (10-minute minimum at each step).
- Evacuation zone: 200 m minimum radius established and enforced.
- Hold period: ≥4 hours at test pressure with no pressure drop >1% (uncorrected).
- Leak detection by approved method (bubble solution, acoustic, gas detector).

REJECTION:
- Pneumatic test pressure >1.10 × MOP — REJECT; immediately vent to safe level;
  test is invalid; risk of catastrophic failure.
- Pneumatic test applied in Class 3 or Class 4 — REJECT; not permitted;
  hydrostatic test required.
- Personnel within 200 m during pressurization above 80% of test pressure —
  REJECT test; evacuate immediately; safety violation.
- No documented engineering justification for pneumatic test in lieu of
  hydrostatic — REJECT test; require hydrostatic test.
- Hold period <4 hours — REJECT; extend or retest.

CLAUSE 13 — LEAK SURVEY — GAS DISTRIBUTION SYSTEMS
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 851 / Table 851-1]

Gas distribution systems (شبكات توزيع الغاز) require periodic leak surveys
(مسح التسربات) to detect and quantify leaks before they reach hazardous
concentrations. Survey frequency per B31.8 Table 851-1 (distribution mains
— خطوط التوزيع الرئيسية):
  - Business districts (مناطق تجارية): survey at least annually (once per year)
    using a gas-detecting instrument capable of measuring 10% of LEL.
  - All other areas: survey at least once every 3 years using approved methods.
  - Known leak-prone pipe materials (bare steel, uncoated cast iron, vintage PE):
    annual survey at minimum.
  - Service lines in business districts: annual survey.
Survey instruments must be calibrated before each survey day. Flame ionization
detectors (FID — كاشف التأين اللهبي) and photo-ionization detectors (PID) are
acceptable; catalytic bead sensors calibrated to methane are also used.
Inspectors must maintain survey maps (خرائط المسح) showing route walked, GPS
track, and any indications found, classified per the operator's leak classification
procedure (leak Grade 1 = immediate hazard; Grade 2 = non-immediate but requires
repair; Grade 3 = non-hazardous, monitor).

ACCEPTANCE:
- Business district mains: surveyed annually with calibrated instrument.
- Other areas: surveyed at minimum every 3 years.
- Survey instrument calibration: performed daily before use, within 6-month
  calibration interval by certified lab.
- Survey records (سجلات المسح): GPS route logged, all indications documented
  with location, concentration, classification.
- Grade 1 leaks (>80% LEL in confined space or ≥1% gas in building): repaired
  immediately or emergency isolation applied before leaving site.
- Grade 2 leaks: repair scheduled within 30 days (or per company procedure,
  maximum 12 months with quarterly monitoring).

REJECTION:
- Business district survey interval >12 months — REJECT program; schedule
  immediate make-up survey.
- Non-business district survey interval >3 years — REJECT; immediate survey
  required.
- Instrument used without current calibration certificate — REJECT survey
  results; resurvey with calibrated instrument.
- Grade 1 leak (immediate hazard) left unrepaired without emergency isolation —
  IMMEDIATE STOP WORK; notify emergency response; evacuate area.
- Survey route not GPS-tracked or walking routes unverifiable — REJECT records;
  resurvey.

CLAUSE 14 — ODORIZATION REQUIREMENTS
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 860 / DOT Part 192.625 (harmonized)]

Natural gas (الغاز الطبيعي) is odorless in its natural state and must be odorized
(إضافة مادة رائحة) so that it can be detected by smell alone before reaching a
dangerous concentration. B31.8 Para. 860 requires that the odorant concentration
be sufficient to make the gas detectable (بالشم فقط) at a concentration of NOT
LESS THAN 1% by volume in air — this corresponds to 20% of the lower explosive
limit (LEL) of methane (5% by volume), providing a safety factor of 5× before
reaching the explosive range. The standard odorant is Tertiary Butyl Mercaptan
(TBM) or a blend (commonly ethyl mercaptan — إيثيل مركبتان) at a typical
injection rate of 1 pound per million cubic feet (lb/MMSCF) of gas, though the
exact rate depends on the specific odorant compound used. Odorization equipment
(wick-type, drip-type, or bypass-type) must be inspected and maintained at
regular intervals. Odorant inventory (مخزون المادة المعطرة) must be tracked.
Gas not required to be odorized: gas used in industrial processes where odorant
would contaminate the product, or gas in Class 1 gathering lines not in proximity
to populated areas (with regulatory approval).

ACCEPTANCE:
- Gas detectable by smell at 1% by volume in air — verified by sniff test or
  odorant concentration measurement instrument.
- Odorant injection rate calibrated to achieve ≥1% detectability; typical
  TBM rate ≥0.5 lb/MMSCF (confirm by odorant supplier data sheet for specific
  compound used).
- Odorization equipment inspected and maintained per operator's O&M manual —
  minimum monthly visual inspection of injection rate.
- Odorant level in storage tank ≥ 15-day supply at current injection rate.
- Gas samples tested for odorant concentration at representative monitoring
  points at least quarterly.

REJECTION:
- Gas odor undetectable (لا رائحة) at 1% by volume in air — REJECT gas supply;
  shut down distribution until odorization is restored and verified.
- Odorization equipment failed or empty — REJECT; emergency isolation of
  affected distribution segment required.
- Odorant injection rate below minimum detectable threshold per compound data
  sheet — REJECT; adjust rate, re-verify detectability.
- No odorant monitoring records for >90 days — REJECT program; require immediate
  inspection and odorant verification.
- Any distribution system operating without odorization in Class 2, 3, or 4
  areas without documented regulatory waiver — REJECT; immediate violation.

CLAUSE 15 — PRESSURE RELIEF AND OVERPRESSURE PROTECTION
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 845 / 845.1 / 845.2]

Every pipeline system operating at a pressure that could create a hazard must be
equipped with pressure-relieving (أجهزة تخفيف الضغط) or pressure-limiting devices
to prevent the operating pressure from exceeding the MAOP by more than the
amounts specified in B31.8 Para. 845.1:
  - For MAOP ≤15 psig: maximum allowable overpressure = 6 psi above MAOP.
  - For MAOP 16 to 60 psig: overpressure ≤ 10% above MAOP.
  - For MAOP 61 to 100 psig: overpressure ≤ 10% above MAOP.
  - For MAOP 101 to 400 psig: overpressure ≤ 10% above MAOP.
  - For MAOP >400 psig: overpressure ≤ 10% above MAOP.
  - In all cases: the relieving pressure shall not exceed 110% of MAOP.
Pressure safety valves (PSV — صمامات أمان الضغط) must be sized to relieve the
full delivery capacity of the upstream source. Pressure regulators (منظمات
الضغط) used as the primary protection must have a monitoring (slam-shut) device
or backup regulator in series. All pressure relief devices must be tested and
inspected at intervals not exceeding 5 years (Para. 845.2) or per manufacturer
recommendation if more frequent.

ACCEPTANCE:
- PSV set pressure: ≤110% of MAOP. (e.g., MAOP = 1000 psig → PSV set ≤1100 psig)
- PSV capacity: capable of passing full upstream flow at set + 10% overpressure.
- Slam-shut valve (SSV — صمام الإغلاق الفوري) or monitoring regulator installed
  in series with pressure regulators per Para. 845.1(b).
- PSV tested and documented: within previous 5 years (or manufacturer interval).
- Vent stack (مدخنة التهوية) discharge: directed to safe location, ≥3 m above
  highest adjacent structure, no ignition sources within 15 m.

REJECTION:
- PSV set pressure >110% of MAOP — REJECT; adjust set point or replace valve.
- PSV capacity insufficient to handle full upstream flow — REJECT; size and
  replace with adequate capacity.
- PSV test overdue (>5 years since last test) — REJECT; tag out and test before
  continued operation.
- No PSV or slam-shut protection on distribution station outlet — REJECT;
  immediate installation required before operation.
- PSV vent stack discharge directed toward building, personnel area, or ignition
  source — REJECT; re-route immediately.

CLAUSE 16 — PATROLLING FREQUENCY — GAS TRANSMISSION LINES
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 851.2 / Table 851-2]

Gas transmission pipelines (خطوط النقل) must be patrolled (دوريات المراقبة) at
regular intervals to observe surface conditions, detect leaks, observe construction
activity near the ROW (حق العبور), and check for unauthorized encroachment. Per
B31.8 Table 851-2, minimum patrolling frequency:
  - Class 1 locations: patrol at least once every 3 weeks (approximately every
    15 calendar days — not exceeding 3 calendar weeks).
  - Class 2 locations: patrol at least once every 2 weeks.
  - Class 3 locations: patrol at least once every 1 week (7 days).
  - Class 4 locations: patrol at least once every 4 days.
Patrol method may be aerial, vehicle, or foot — aerial is most common for
long-distance transmission lines. Patrol records must include the patrol date,
method, route, findings, and name of the patrol person. Third-party damage
(الضرر من الأطراف الثالثة — داماج) is the leading cause of transmission pipeline
incidents; patrol frequency directly addresses this risk. Any patrol finding of
encroachment, excavation near ROW, or ground movement must be reported within
24 hours.

ACCEPTANCE:
- Class 1: patrol interval ≤ 21 calendar days (3 weeks).
- Class 2: patrol interval ≤ 14 calendar days (2 weeks).
- Class 3: patrol interval ≤ 7 calendar days (1 week).
- Class 4: patrol interval ≤ 4 calendar days.
- Patrol records: date, method, route, name, findings — retained ≥5 years.
- Any third-party activity within 30 m of pipeline ROW reported to management
  within 24 hours of detection.

REJECTION:
- Patrol interval exceeds maximum allowed for the location class — REJECT
  patrol program; schedule make-up patrol immediately and increase frequency.
- No patrol records maintained — REJECT; program non-compliant; require
  immediate documentation system.
- Patrolman not qualified or unfamiliar with the patrol route and emergency
  procedures — REJECT; retrain before returning to patrol duty.
- Third-party excavation within 1 m of pipeline found during patrol — IMMEDIATE
  STOP WORK on excavation; verify pipeline integrity before allowing resumption.

CLAUSE 17 — MAOP REDUCTION AND DE-RATING CRITERIA
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 845.3 / 841.3 / 855]

A pipeline operator must reduce MAOP (تخفيض أقصى ضغط التشغيل المسموح) when
the location class has changed (population increase), a defect has been found
that reduces the effective wall thickness or yield strength, or the design
documentation cannot support the current operating pressure. Para. 845.3 requires
that when location class changes from Class 1 to Class 2, Class 3, or Class 4,
the operator must within 24 months either: (a) pressure-test the line to the
new class test pressure requirement, (b) physically upgrade the line
(replace pipe, increase wall thickness), OR (c) reduce MAOP to a value
consistent with the new class design factor. MAOP de-rating formula:
  MAOP_new = 2 × S × t / D × F_new × E × T
where F_new corresponds to the new (higher) class. Loss of traceability of pipe
grade (SMYS unknown) forces use of SMYS = Grade B minimum (35,000 psi) in the
calculation — a significant MAOP reduction for high-strength pipe of unknown
grade. ILI results showing wall loss >10% of nominal wall thickness require an
engineering fitness-for-service (FFS — الصلاحية للخدمة) assessment before
maintaining MAOP.

ACCEPTANCE:
- MAOP re-calculated using correct F for current location class.
- If class upgraded: operator response (test, upgrade, or de-rate) completed
  within 24 months of class change determination.
- Fitness-for-service assessment per ASME B31G or RSTRENG (modified B31G)
  accepted as engineering basis for maintaining MAOP with corrosion defects.
- Pipe grade unknown: SMYS assumed = 35,000 psi (Grade B minimum) and MAOP
  calculated accordingly.
- All MAOP changes recorded and communicated to SCADA (السكادا) operating team.

REJECTION:
- MAOP not reduced within 24 months of confirmed location class change —
  REJECT operating basis; mandatory pressure reduction until compliance.
- ILI results showing remaining wall <87.5% of nominal (>12.5% wall loss) with
  no FFS assessment — REJECT continued operation at current MAOP; reduce pressure
  or repair immediately.
- MAOP maintained using higher-class pipe grade without MTR to prove it —
  REJECT; assume lowest grade (Grade B) until certified MTRs recovered.
- Operator has no documented MAOP change management procedure — REJECT
  integrity management program as incomplete.

CLAUSE 18 — IN-LINE INSPECTION (ILI) IN HIGH CONSEQUENCE AREAS
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 855 / Appendix P — Integrity Management / CFR Part 192 Subpart O]

High Consequence Areas (HCA — مناطق العواقب الوخيمة) are defined as areas where
a potential pipeline failure could cause significant harm to people or the
environment — including Class 3 and Class 4 locations, certain Class 2 areas,
specific identified sites (schools, hospitals, commercial buildings occupied by
50+ persons), and unusually sensitive ecological areas (مناطق بيئية حساسة).
B31.8 Appendix P (Integrity Management Program for Gas Transmission Pipelines)
requires baseline assessment of all HCA segments. In-line inspection (ILI) using
intelligent pigs (الخنازير الذكية — الرسالة الداخلية) is the preferred baseline
assessment method when the pipeline is piggable. ILI tool types:
  - Magnetic Flux Leakage (MFL — تفريغ الفيض المغناطيسي): detects metal loss
    (corrosion — تآكل), pitting, general wall loss.
  - Ultrasonic Testing (UT pig — خنزير UT): detects wall loss and cracking.
  - Caliper pig (قياس الأبعاد): detects geometry anomalies — dents, ovality, wrinkles.
  - Crack Detection Tool (CDT): detects SCC (stress corrosion cracking — تشقق
    الإجهاد التآكلي) and seam weld anomalies.
ILI must be performed by a qualified ILI vendor (مورد ILI مؤهل) and results
interpreted by qualified analysts meeting NACE/AMPP or company standards.

ACCEPTANCE:
- Baseline ILI completed within 7 years of initial HCA designation per Appendix P.
- ILI tool specification (MFL or UT) appropriate for threat type (corrosion vs.
  cracking vs. geometry).
- ILI tool performance specification: MFL tool minimum detection threshold ≤10%
  wall loss for metal loss; sizing accuracy ±10% wall at 80% confidence.
- All ILI-identified anomalies with estimated failure pressure <1.10 × MAOP:
  immediate excavation and evaluation required.
- Anomalies with estimated failure pressure 1.10–1.25 × MAOP: excavation and
  repair within 60 days.
- Annual reassessment of HCA segments for new threats; full ILI re-run at
  intervals not exceeding 7 years.

REJECTION:
- Baseline ILI not completed for piggable HCA segment within 7-year window —
  REJECT IMP; mandatory completion before next operating year.
- ILI tool run without pre-run geometry/caliper pass — REJECT ILI data if tool
  geometry issues (dents, bore restrictions) could have impacted data quality.
- Anomaly with failure pressure <1.10 × MAOP not actioned within 5 days —
  REJECT operation at current MAOP; reduce pressure to bring failure pressure
  above 1.10 × reduced MAOP.
- ILI vendor not meeting qualification criteria — REJECT ILI results; repeat
  with qualified vendor.
- No ILI re-assessment within 7 years on HCA segment — REJECT continued
  operation without pressure reduction or alternative assessment.

CLAUSE 19 — INTEGRITY MANAGEMENT PROGRAM (IMP) — BASELINE AND RE-ASSESSMENT
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. Appendix P / P-5 / P-6 / P-7]

The B31.8 Integrity Management Program (برنامج إدارة النزاهة) for gas transmission
pipelines requires operators to systematically identify HCA segments, assess the
relevant threats (التهديدات), evaluate and reduce risks, and confirm the pipeline's
continued fitness-for-service. The IMP must be documented, reviewed annually, and
audited. Baseline assessment must be completed within 10 years of the IMP effective
date (or 7 years for HCA segments identified from the initial analysis) using at
least one of: ILI, pressure test, or direct assessment (DA — التقييم المباشر).
Reassessment intervals are threat-dependent:
  - Internal corrosion: ≤7 years between ILI runs or DIA (direct internal
    assessment) inspections, or more frequently if corrosion rate data warrants.
  - External corrosion: ≤7 years between ECDA or ILI runs.
  - SCC: ≤7 years between SCC-DA or crack ILI runs.
  - Third-party damage: reassess after significant events; no fixed time interval
    but patrol and DA must be ongoing.
  - Manufacturing defects: reassess per original design pressure test or ILI results.
Threat interaction (تفاعل التهديدات) must be considered — e.g., SCC is more likely
where cathodic protection is inadequate AND high-pH coating disbondment occurs.

ACCEPTANCE:
- IMP document: current edition, reviewed within last 12 months, signed by
  responsible engineer (مهندس مسؤول).
- HCA identification: updated within 12 months using current population data
  and aerial/satellite imagery.
- Baseline assessment completed for all HCA segments within required window.
- Reassessment intervals: ≤7 years for corrosion and SCC threats in HCA.
- Performance metrics tracked: number of leaks, ruptures, ILI anomalies found
  vs. predicted, repair completion timeliness — reviewed annually.
- IMP audit (تدقيق البرنامج): internal audit annually; external/regulatory audit
  per jurisdiction schedule.

REJECTION:
- IMP not updated within 2 years — REJECT; treat as non-compliant; update
  immediately before regulatory audit.
- HCA segments not identified using current (≤12-month-old) population data —
  REJECT; update HCA identification.
- Baseline assessment overdue for any HCA segment — REJECT IMP as complete;
  conduct assessment immediately.
- Reassessment interval exceeded without risk-based justification accepted by
  regulator — REJECT; mandate assessment within 90 days.
- No performance metrics tracked — REJECT IMP as lacking continuous improvement
  process; implement metrics tracking.

CLAUSE 20 — REPAIR CRITERIA — DENTS, GOUGES, AND CORROSION DEFECTS
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 851.4 / Appendix P / ASME B31G / API 579-1/ASME FFS-1]

Field repairs on in-service gas pipelines must follow documented engineering
criteria. Key rejectable defect thresholds:

DENTS (انبعاجات):
  - Plain dent (no metal loss) in body: depth >6% of outside diameter (OD) —
    REJECT; requires immediate repair.
  - Dent affecting top 1/3 of pipe (上方 — overhead arc): depth >2% OD if the
    dent is at 10-to-2 o'clock position — REJECT; higher risk of rebound cracking.
  - Dent with stress concentrators (gouges, weld, seam, corrosion within dent):
    ANY depth with associated damage — REJECT immediately.
  - Kinked or buckled pipe: any visible kink — REJECT.

CORROSION (تآكل) — Metal Loss:
  - Assess per ASME B31G (original method) or Modified B31G (RSTRENG):
  - If remaining wall thickness < 80% of nominal (>20% wall loss) in a localized
    area: REJECT per B31G; repair or FFS assessment required.
  - Safe maximum operating pressure (SMOP) from B31G: if SMOP < 1.10 × MAOP —
    REJECT; repair or reduce MAOP.
  - Corrosion length criterion: if (L²/Dt) ≤ 20 (dimensionless), use B31G
    area criterion; if (L²/Dt) > 20, SMOP = MAOP × (1 - d/t) — simplified.

GOUGES (خدوش معدنية):
  - Metal loss due to mechanical damage with reduced wall thickness: evaluate as
    corrosion per B31G; if stress raiser suspected (cold work, cracking),
    perform MT or PT before accepting.
  - Gouge depth >12.5% of nominal wall (mill tolerance) — REJECT; evaluate
    and repair.

ACCEPTANCE:
- Plain dents: depth ≤6% OD in pipe body (excluding dents with stress raisers).
- Body dents with no associated damage at 4-to-8 o'clock position: ≤6% OD.
- Corrosion: remaining wall ≥80% of nominal AND SMOP per B31G ≥1.10 × MAOP.
- Gouge: depth ≤12.5% of nominal wall with no evidence of cold work or cracking.
- All accepted defects: documented with dimensions, location, UT wall readings,
  and B31G calculation in the inspection record.

REJECTION:
- Dent depth >6% OD — REJECT; repair by cut-out, full encirclement sleeve, or
  approved composite repair per ASME B31.8 Appendix R.
- Any dent with associated gouge, corrosion, crack, or weld — REJECT regardless
  of depth; immediate repair.
- B31G SMOP < 1.10 × MAOP — REJECT; reduce MAOP or repair before continued
  operation.
- Gouge depth >12.5% nominal wall — REJECT; evaluate by NDE for cracking; repair.
- Any visible crack in pipe body — REJECT; cut-out mandatory; no in-situ repair
  of cracks permitted under B31.8.

CLAUSE 21 — ABANDONMENT REQUIREMENTS
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 853 / 853.1 / 853.2]

When a gas pipeline segment is permanently taken out of service (يُغلق نهائياً —
هجر الخط)، the operator must perform abandonment procedures (إجراءات الهجر) to
ensure safety and environmental protection. Para. 853.1 requires that the
pipeline be disconnected from all sources of gas and purged (تنقية — تطهير) of
gas using an inert gas (nitrogen — نيتروجين) or water until combustible gas
content is ≤10% of LEL at the venting point. Cathodic protection (الحماية
الكاثودية) on the abandoned segment does not need to be maintained after
abandonment if the pipeline no longer contains gas; however, interference
effects on adjacent active pipelines must be checked. Inspection requirements
for abandoned lines: no ongoing inspection obligation unless the abandoned
segment physically affects an adjacent active pipeline. All valves on the
abandoned segment must be physically removed or physically locked in the closed
position and tagged. The abandonment must be documented with a record of the
abandonment date, the purging method, the final gas-free verification reading,
and the record filed in the pipeline records permanently.

ACCEPTANCE:
- Purging complete: combustible gas concentration ≤10% of LEL at vent endpoints
  — confirmed by calibrated gas detector (كاشف الغاز المعاير).
- Physical isolation: all tie-in valves closed and locked; blind flanges (شفاة
  عمياء) or pipe caps installed at all connection points.
- CP system: interference check on adjacent active pipelines completed post-
  abandonment; stray current (<1 mV/m² on adjacent pipe) confirmed acceptable.
- Abandonment record: filed permanently in pipeline records with purging data,
  isolation as-built, and gas-free verification.
- Regulatory notification: abandonment reported to applicable authority within
  required timeframe (30 days typical in most jurisdictions).

REJECTION:
- Pipeline physically isolated but gas concentration >10% LEL at vent — REJECT
  abandonment as incomplete; continue purging.
- No physical isolation (blind or cap) — REJECT; connection to gas source
  remains; must be physically severed.
- CP interference on adjacent active pipeline not checked — REJECT; conduct
  close-interval potential survey (CIPS — مسح التدرج الكاثودي) on adjacent lines
  within 90 days.
- Abandonment records not retained — REJECT; records are permanent; reconstruct
  from field logs and retain.
- Abandoned segment left partially filled with gas without purging >10% LEL —
  REJECT; immediate re-purge required.

CLAUSE 22 — GAS DISTRIBUTION SERVICE LINE INSPECTION
[REF: ASME B31.8 Para. 857 / 855.5 / Table 857-1]

Gas service lines (خطوط الخدمة — خطوط الاتصال) are the small-diameter piping
connecting the distribution main to the customer meter assembly. B31.8 Para. 857
requires that each service line be inspected at the time of installation and
periodically thereafter. Inspection includes:
  - Pressure test at installation: service lines NPS 2 and smaller must be tested
    at a minimum of 90 psig (620 kPa) or 1.5 × MOP, whichever is greater, for
    a minimum of 15 minutes with no pressure drop.
  - Periodic inspection: each service line must be visually inspected and leak-
    checked at a minimum whenever the meter is changed, when service is
    interrupted and restored, and whenever any construction activity disturbs the
    ground within 1 m of the service line.
  - Leakage survey: service lines in business districts — annual leak survey
    (same as mains per Clause 13).
  - Service line materials: steel, copper, or approved PE (polyethylene — البولي
    إيثيلين المعتمد) per ASTM D2513 / PE 2708 or PE 4710. Galvanized pipe not
    permitted in gas service (Para. 841.32). Copper not permitted if gas contains
    >0.3 grains H₂S per 100 SCF (sour gas — غاز حامضي).
  - All service lines through building foundations or walls: metallic sleeve or
    equivalent protection, mechanically protected from settlement.

ACCEPTANCE:
- Installation pressure test: ≥90 psig OR ≥1.5 × MOP (whichever is greater)
  for ≥15 minutes with zero pressure drop.
- Pipe material: steel, copper (sweet gas only), or PE per ASTM D2513.
- PE pipe: tracer wire (سلك الكشف) or locating system installed alongside for
  locatability.
- Annual leak survey completed for service lines in business districts.
- Service lines inspected at each meter change and after any nearby excavation.
- Through-wall penetrations: approved sleeve with mechanical protection installed.

REJECTION:
- Installation test pressure < 90 psig or < 1.5 × MOP — REJECT; retest to
  correct pressure before burying.
- Any pressure drop during 15-minute test — REJECT; locate and repair leak;
  retest entire service line.
- Galvanized pipe installed in gas service — REJECT; remove and replace with
  approved material.
- Copper pipe used on gas containing >0.3 grains H₂S/100 SCF — REJECT; replace
  with steel.
- PE service line installed without tracer wire — REJECT; excavate and install
  tracer wire; re-backfill.
- Service line not protected through building foundation — REJECT; install sleeve
  before gas-up.
- Meter changed without post-change leak check — REJECT; perform immediate leak
  check before leaving site.
