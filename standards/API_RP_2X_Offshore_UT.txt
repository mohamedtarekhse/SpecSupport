CLAUSE 1 — SCOPE OF API RP 2X
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 1 — Scope]

API RP 2X establishes minimum requirements for ultrasonic examination (UT) and
magnetic particle examination (MT) of welds and base metal in offshore structural
fabrication (التفتيش على اللحامات والهياكل البحرية). The standard applies to
primary structural components including jacket legs (أرجل الجاكيت), horizontal
and diagonal braces (الدعامات الأفقية والقطرية), deck framing members (أعضاء
تأطير السطح), nodal connections (وصلات العقد), and tubular joint welds (لحامات
الوصلات الأنبوبية) in both T, Y, and K configurations. API RP 2X covers both
pre-weld base metal examination for laminations and post-weld UT of completed
joint welds. The standard is mandatory for all API RP 2A-WSD and API RP 2A-LRFD
governed structures where structural integrity of nodal connections is critical to
platform life. Examination shall be applied to all full-penetration groove welds
in primary structural members and all partial-penetration welds where specified
by engineering design documents (وثائق التصميم الهندسي). Secondary structural
welds are examined per project-specific inspection and test plans (ITP) referenced
against this standard.

ACCEPTANCE: UT and MT performed on all primary structural welds per scope in
Section 1. Coverage of 100% of weld volume required for full-penetration joints.

REJECTION: Any primary weld excluded from UT/MT scope without documented
engineering justification is non-conforming. Coverage below 100% for
full-penetration joints is rejectable unless geometric inaccessibility is
documented and alternative method approved by Owner.

CLAUSE 2 — PERSONNEL QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 5 — Qualification of UT Technicians]

API RP 2X mandates a minimum of Level II qualification (مستوى ثانٍ) for all
personnel performing and evaluating UT examinations on offshore structural welds.
Level I technicians (مستوى أول) may perform scanning operations ONLY under the
direct supervision of a certified Level II or Level III, and may NOT make
accept/reject evaluations independently. Level II technicians must be qualified
specifically in the shear-wave angle-beam techniques applicable to tubular and
plate weld geometries (تقنيات الموجة القاطعة الزاوية للأنبوب والصفيحة). Qualification
may be achieved through the API 2X written and practical examination process or
through an employer-based program that meets the requirements of ASNT SNT-TC-1A
or ASNT CP-189, subject to the Owner's written approval. For nodal weld UT on
complex geometry (T, Y, K, X joints), the Level II technician must have
documented experience on minimum 3 similar joint configurations (وثائق تجربة
مثبتة) as part of their qualification record. Level III oversight (إشراف مستوى
ثالث) is required for procedure approval, technique qualification, and resolution
of borderline indications.

ACCEPTANCE: Level II minimum, certified per API 2X Section 5 or approved ASNT
program. Experience documented on minimum 3 offshore tubular joint configurations
for nodal work. Level III on-site or available for consultation within 24 hours.

REJECTION: Level I personnel making accept/reject decisions without Level II
direct supervision. Any technician without documented experience in angle-beam
shear-wave UT on tubular geometry performing nodal weld examination. Expired
certification (>3 years without renewal) is rejectable.

CLAUSE 3 — WRITTEN UT PROCEDURE REQUIREMENTS
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 6 — Written Procedure Requirements]

A written UT examination procedure (الإجراء الكتابي المعتمد للفحص بالموجات فوق
الصوتية) shall be prepared and qualified prior to commencement of production
examination. The procedure shall be reviewed and approved by a Level III UT
technician and accepted by the Owner's authorized inspection representative. The
written procedure must address, at minimum: material type and thickness range,
weld joint configuration and access, transducer frequency and size, scanning
index point verification, beam angles to be used, calibration block type and
method, DAC construction method, scanning pattern and direction, recording
threshold, and accept/reject criteria referencing the applicable API 2X table.
Procedure qualification (تأهيل الإجراء) shall be demonstrated on a mockup or
qualification block representative of the production weld geometry and thickness
range within ±25% of nominal. Any change to equipment make/model, frequency,
beam angle, or couplant type outside the qualified range constitutes a
procedure revision requiring re-qualification and Owner approval before use.
All procedures shall be uniquely numbered and revision-controlled per the
fabricator's document control system.

ACCEPTANCE: Written procedure approved by Level III, accepted by Owner
representative. Qualified on representative mockup within ±25% of production
thickness. All 12 minimum elements addressed in the procedure document.

REJECTION: Examination commenced without approved written procedure. Procedure
applied outside its qualified thickness range without documented justification.
Procedure changes made in field without Level III review and Owner acceptance.

CLAUSE 4 — EQUIPMENT FREQUENCY SELECTION
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 7 — Equipment and Transducers]

Transducer frequency selection (اختيار تردد المحول الكهرضغطي) for offshore
structural steel examination shall be based on material thickness, grain structure,
and weld geometry. For structural plate welds (لحامات الصفائح الهيكلية) with
thickness from 6 mm to 25 mm, a frequency of 5 MHz is generally recommended to
maximize near-surface resolution and sensitivity to fine planar defects. For
plate thicknesses from 25 mm to 75 mm, frequencies of 2.25 MHz or 4 MHz shall be
used to balance penetration with resolution. For thicknesses exceeding 75 mm,
such as chord walls in large-diameter jacket legs (جدران وتر أرجل الجاكيت),
2.25 MHz or 1 MHz transducers may be required to achieve adequate sound
penetration, subject to demonstrated DAC construction at full skip depth. Dual-
element (pitch-catch) transducers (المحولات الثنائية) shall be used for near-
surface lamination detection at frequencies of 4–5 MHz. Single-element contact
transducers with piezoelectric crystals of 10 mm x 10 mm to 25 mm x 25 mm face
size are standard for structural weld scanning. Phased array (المصفوفة المتدرجة)
and TOFD systems may be used as supplements where approved by the written
procedure and Owner, but shall not replace the calibrated contact UT baseline
unless fully documented and validated per API 2X Annex requirements.

ACCEPTANCE: 5 MHz for 6–25 mm plate. 2.25 or 4 MHz for 25–75 mm. 1 or 2.25 MHz
for >75 mm. Crystal face within 10–25 mm square or equivalent area. DAC
demonstrable at full examination depth with selected frequency.

REJECTION: Use of 10 MHz or higher on sections >25 mm thick (signal dropout risk).
Frequency change from procedure-qualified value without Level III authorization.
Any transducer with cracked, delaminated, or abraded face. Uncalibrated
frequency.

CLAUSE 5 — SURFACE PREPARATION FOR UT ACCESS
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 8 — Surface Preparation]

Surface preparation (تجهيز السطح) for contact UT on offshore structural welds
shall ensure that the examination surface allows full acoustic coupling between
transducer and base metal without signal loss due to surface irregularity, weld
spatter, rust, or mill scale. The weld crown (قمة اللحام) shall be dressed flush
with the base metal surface using angle grinding (جلخ زاوية) to within ±0.5 mm
of flush when weld cap geometry prevents proper transducer placement or blocks
the required beam paths. Surface roughness (خشونة السطح) shall not exceed
Ra 6.3 µm (250 µin) on the scanning surface within the full one-skip-distance
zone on each side of the weld. For tubular welds with convex geometry (الهندسة
المحدبة), the OD scanning surface shall be dressed to accommodate flat transducer
contact over a minimum of 50% of the transducer face. Weld backing bars (قضبان
الدعم الخلفي) and run-on/run-off tabs shall be removed and the area ground smooth
prior to UT where access is required. Couplant (مادة التوصيل الصوتي) shall be
glycerin, cellulose gel, or equivalent water-soluble paste; water alone is
acceptable only on vertical or overhead surfaces where gel is impractical.
Application of protective coatings, paint, or primer over the scan path prior to
UT completion is prohibited.

ACCEPTANCE: Weld crown dressed to within ±0.5 mm flush. Scanning surface
Ra ≤ 6.3 µm. Transducer face contact ≥50% on tubular OD. Approved couplant
applied uniformly. Backing bars removed from all UT access zones.

REJECTION: Weld cap protruding >0.5 mm beyond flush in transducer travel zone.
Loose mill scale, heavy rust, or weld spatter under scan path. Paint or primer
applied before UT complete. Any surface condition causing >6 dB scan-to-scan
amplitude variation during transfer loss verification.

CLAUSE 6 — CALIBRATION BLOCKS AND REFERENCE REFLECTORS
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 9 — Calibration]

Calibration (المعايرة) for angle-beam shear-wave UT on offshore structural welds
shall use reference blocks (كتل المرجعية) fabricated from steel with acoustic
velocity within ±0.005 in/µs of the production material, with hardness ≤ HRC 25
to prevent material property mismatch. Side-drilled holes (SDH — الثقوب المحفورة
جانبياً) are the primary reference reflectors for DAC construction in structural
weld UT per API RP 2X; SDH diameter shall be 1/16 inch (1.6 mm) for examination
of weld cross-sections less than or equal to 1 inch (25.4 mm) thick, and 3/32
inch (2.4 mm) for thicknesses from 1 inch to 2 inches (25.4–50.8 mm). Flat-
bottom holes (FBH — الثقوب ذات القاع المسطح) are used as alternative reference
reflectors for normal-beam (0°) longitudinal wave calibration on base metal
lamination checks, with FBH diameter equal to the greater of 3/16 inch (4.8 mm)
or as specified by the project specification. IIW Type 1 (V1) blocks (كتلة IIW
النوع الأول) are used for beam angle verification, index point determination, and
velocity measurement before each examination session. ROMPAS or equivalent
distance-amplitude correction (DAC) blocks shall be fabricated for each thickness
range examined, with the SDH at minimum three depth positions: ¼T, ½T, and ¾T of
the section under examination. Calibration shall be performed at ambient
temperature; if steel surface temperature during production examination deviates
by more than ±25°F (±14°C) from calibration temperature, re-calibration is
required.

ACCEPTANCE: IIW block used for angle/index verification each session. SDH
diameter 1.6 mm for T ≤ 25.4 mm, 2.4 mm for 25.4–50.8 mm. DAC block covers
¼T, ½T, ¾T depth positions. Calibration within ±25°F of production surface
temperature. Material acoustic velocity match within ±0.005 in/µs.

REJECTION: Use of incorrect SDH diameter for the thickness range. DAC block
material acoustic velocity outside tolerance. Calibration verification skipped
at session start or after instrument change. Any instrument setting altered
after calibration without documented re-verification.

CLAUSE 7 — DAC CONSTRUCTION FOR STRUCTURAL STEEL
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 10 — Distance-Amplitude Correction (DAC)]

Distance-amplitude correction curves (منحنيات تصحيح البُعد والسعة — DAC) shall
be constructed for each angle-beam transducer, frequency, and thickness range used
in production examination of offshore structural welds. DAC shall be constructed
using a minimum of three points obtained from the SDH at ¼T, ½T, and ¾T of the
applicable reference block thickness. These three peak amplitudes are connected by
a smooth curve to form the primary DAC reference level (100% DAC). The DAC curve
and all examination gains shall be set such that the maximum SDH signal from the
reference block is at 80% of full screen height (FSH) when used as the primary
reference sensitivity, consistent with the AWS D1.1 amplitude evaluation method
referenced in API 2X Appendix for plate butt weld comparison. All recording
thresholds and accept/reject decisions are expressed as a percentage of the DAC
amplitude at the applicable metal path. For computerized UT instruments with
digital DAC storage, the DAC display shall be validated against the analog
peak-hold trace on the reference block before each production session. Software-
applied DAC curves are acceptable when the instrument is demonstrated to correlate
with manual DAC within ±2 dB across the full depth range. Transfer correction
(تصحيح النقل) between reference block and production weld surface shall be
measured and recorded at the start of each weld examined; a transfer loss >6 dB
shall be investigated before proceeding.

ACCEPTANCE: Three-point DAC minimum (¼T, ½T, ¾T). Primary reference set to
80% FSH from SDH. Digital DAC validated within ±2 dB of manual. Transfer
correction measured each weld. Transfer correction ≤6 dB applied as gain
compensation.

REJECTION: DAC constructed from fewer than three depth points. Primary
reference amplitude above 100% FSH or below 60% FSH (out-of-scale or under-
sensitive). Transfer correction >6 dB applied without investigation of cause.
DAC not re-established after transducer replacement or instrument re-boot during
session.

CLAUSE 8 — SCANNING PATTERNS FOR PLATE BUTT WELDS
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 11 — Scanning Patterns — Plate Butt Welds]

Angle-beam UT scanning (نمط المسح بالموجة الزاوية) for full-penetration plate butt
welds (لحامات الربط المستعرضة في الصفائح) on offshore structures shall be
performed from both sides of the weld centerline (خط المركز) using a minimum of
two refracted shear-wave angles: 45° and 60° (or 45° and 70°) to ensure complete
volumetric coverage of all weld fusion zones and heat-affected zones (HAZ). Each
scan shall progress longitudinally along the weld with transducer oscillation
(تأرجح المحول) of ±10° about the primary scan direction at a maximum scan index
increment of 10 mm (3/8 inch) between parallel scan passes. The transducer shall
not be advanced faster than 6 inches (150 mm) per second during active scanning
to ensure adequate dwell time for indication detection. Transverse scanning
(المسح العرضي — parallel to the weld axis direction) shall also be performed for
detection of transverse cracks (الشقوق العرضية) in all full-penetration butt
welds; this scan is performed with the transducer beam directed toward the weld
centerline at the selected refracted angle while traversing along the weld cap.
For plate thicknesses ≥50 mm, an additional near-surface scan using a 70° angle
or TOFD shall be included to compensate for dead zone limitations near the weld
root and crown. All scanning shall be performed with a calibrated couplant
continuity monitoring method (visual or audible) to prevent loss of coupling
during examination.

ACCEPTANCE: Minimum 45° + 60° or 45° + 70° angle coverage from both sides of
weld. Scan increment ≤10 mm (3/8 in). Scan speed ≤150 mm/s. Transducer
oscillation ±10°. Transverse scan performed on all full-penetration butt welds.
Near-surface supplement for T ≥50 mm.

REJECTION: Single-angle scan only without documented justification. Scan
increment >10 mm between passes. Scan speed exceeding 150 mm/s without automatic
encoding to compensate. Transverse scanning omitted on full-penetration welds.

CLAUSE 9 — SCANNING PATTERNS FOR T-JOINT AND K-JOINT TUBULAR WELDS
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 12 — Scanning Patterns — Tubular Connections]

T-joint and K-joint tubular weld UT (فحص لحامات الوصلات الأنبوبية على شكل T
وK) requires adapted scanning techniques due to the curved geometry of the brace
(الدعامة) outer surface and the variable weld bevel orientation relative to the
chord (الوتر). For T-joints, angle-beam scanning shall be performed from the brace
OD at minimum two angles (45° and 60°) scanning toward the chord wall fusion
zone, scanning parallel to the weld seam circumferentially. The transducer shall
be repositioned at circumferential intervals not exceeding 25 mm to maintain full
volume coverage around the full 360° weld circumference. For K-joints (الوصلات
على شكل K), the scan must account for the acute-angle heel (الكعب — compression
side) where weld access is geometrically restricted; a specialized probe shoe
(حذاء المحول المتخصص) contoured to the brace OD shall be fabricated and
demonstrated to achieve >70% transducer face contact before use. The chord wall
scan (المسح من جانب الوتر) shall be performed where brace OD access is restricted,
using normal beam for lamination verification and angle-beam for weld cross-section
coverage. For brace-to-chord intersections where full UT is geometrically
impossible, the inaccessible zone shall be documented, agreed with Owner, and
supplemented by RT or TOFD where practicable. All tubular weld scans shall be
indexed and mapped relative to clock positions (0° to 360° in 30° increments).

ACCEPTANCE: Both 45° and 60° angles used from brace OD. Circumferential
intervals ≤25 mm. Contoured shoe giving ≥70% face contact. 360° circumferential
map indexed at 30° clock positions. Chord-side scan performed where brace OD
blocked.

REJECTION: Single-angle scan only. Circumferential interval >25 mm. Flat
transducer on curved brace OD without contact verification. Any weld zone
excluded without Owner-approved documented justification and alternative
examination plan.

CLAUSE 10 — NODE WELD UT TECHNIQUE — SPECIAL GEOMETRY CONSIDERATIONS
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 13 — Node and Multiplanar Connection UT]

Node welds (لحامات العقد) on offshore jacket structures represent the most
geometrically complex UT challenge in structural fabrication inspection. Nodes are
multi-plane connections where multiple braces intersect a common chord, producing
compound curvature surfaces (أسطح ذات انحناء مركب), variable weld bevel
orientations, and restricted access zones at brace-brace intersections. Before UT
commences on any node configuration, a weld access mapping exercise (تمرين رسم
إمكانية الوصول) shall be completed by the Level II technician, identifying all
fully accessible, partially accessible, and inaccessible weld zones. Specialized
contoured transducer shoes shall be fabricated and qualified for each chord OD
diameter and brace OD diameter pair present in the node, demonstrating acoustic
coupling on the actual fabricated geometry prior to production scanning. Sound
path and beam geometry modeling (محاكاة مسار الصوت والشعاع) is recommended for
all node connections with brace OD-to-chord OD ratio (β ratio) greater than 0.6,
to identify dead zones and multi-path reflection artifacts. Scanning on node welds
shall cover the full accessible weld volume using a minimum of three transducer
angles (45°, 60°, and 70° or TOFD) circumferentially. Any zone where calculated
beam coverage is less than 80% of the weld cross-section volume shall be
documented as a geometric shadow zone and an alternative supplementary technique
approved before sign-off. Node weld inspection records shall include a to-scale
weld map identifying each zone's examination method, coverage percentage, and
indication locations.

ACCEPTANCE: Access mapping completed before UT. Contoured shoes qualified on
actual fabricated geometry. Three angles minimum (45°, 60°, 70°) on all node
welds. Coverage ≥80% weld volume across all accessible zones. Weld map with
examination method documented per zone.

REJECTION: Standard flat probe used without contact verification on node curved
surface. Coverage <80% without Owner approval of alternative. Node UT signed off
without zone-by-zone weld map. β > 0.6 node examined without beam path modeling.

CLAUSE 11 — RECORDING THRESHOLD
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 14 — Recording of Indications]

The recording threshold (عتبة التسجيل) for UT indications (مؤشرات الفحص فوق
الصوتي) in offshore structural welds shall be 50% of the DAC amplitude (50%
DAC) at the applicable metal path depth. Any indication producing a reflected
signal amplitude equal to or exceeding 50% DAC must be recorded in the
examination record with the following minimum data: weld identification number,
position along weld (clock position for tubulars, distance from reference datum
for plate welds), depth of indication below scanning surface, indication length
(using the 6 dB drop method), beam angle used, scan direction (from which side
of weld), and peak amplitude expressed as a percentage of DAC. Indications
between 50% and 100% DAC (below the rejection threshold) must still be
characterized and reported as recordable indications (مؤشرات واجبة التسجيل) in
the final examination report and reviewed by the Level III before final
disposition. The 6 dB drop method (طريقة الانخفاض 6 ديسيبل) shall be used to
measure indication length for all indications at or above 50% DAC. Maximum
scanning sensitivity shall be maintained at +6 dB above primary DAC sensitivity
during scanning to ensure that indications at or near the recording threshold
are not missed due to scan angle variability.

ACCEPTANCE: All indications ≥50% DAC recorded. 6 dB drop length measurement
applied. All required data fields documented per record. Scanning gain set at
+6 dB above reference during scanning sweep. Level III reviews all sub-rejection
recordable indications.

REJECTION: Indications ≥50% DAC not recorded. Length measurement by method other
than 6 dB drop without written justification and Level III approval. Scanning
performed at reference sensitivity without the required +6 dB scanning gain
supplement. Incomplete indication record (missing depth, angle, or position data).

CLAUSE 12 — ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA — AMPLITUDE-BASED ACCEPT/REJECT
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 15 — Acceptance Criteria (Table 15.1)]

Acceptance criteria (معايير القبول) for UT of offshore structural welds per
API RP 2X Table 15.1 are based on a combination of indication amplitude relative
to DAC and indication length. For indications with amplitude LESS THAN 100% DAC
(below DAC level), acceptance is based on the measured indication length relative
to weld thickness (T): indications with amplitude between 50% and 100% DAC and
length ≤ 2/3 of the weld section thickness (T) are ACCEPTABLE without repair for
any weld thickness. For indications with amplitude AT OR ABOVE 100% DAC
(at or exceeding DAC reference): ALL such indications are REJECTABLE regardless
of length, except where the indication is characterized by Level III as a
geometric reflector (false call) with documented evidence. For weld thickness
T ≤ 3/4 inch (19 mm): indications at ≥100% DAC are REJECTED regardless of length.
For T > 3/4 inch (19 mm): indications at ≥100% DAC are REJECTED regardless of
length. Indication length for accept/reject evaluation is determined by 6 dB drop
from peak. The following AWS D1.1 Table 6.2 Class D criteria comparison applies
where referenced in API 2X Appendix C: an indication is rejectable if amplitude
exceeds DAC AND length exceeds allowable per the applicable D1.1 class table for
the weld category. Owner-defined fitness-for-service (FFS) criteria per API 579
may be substituted for API 2X Table 15.1 only with documented Owner and Level III
engineering approval.

ACCEPTANCE: Amplitude <100% DAC AND length ≤ 2/3 T: ACCEPT. Amplitude <50% DAC:
ACCEPT (below recording threshold, no action required). Geometric reflector
confirmed by Level III with documented evidence: ACCEPT with record.

REJECTION: Amplitude ≥100% DAC at any depth or length: REJECT — immediate repair
required (إصلاح فوري مطلوب). Amplitude between 50–100% DAC AND length > 2/3 T:
REJECT. Any indication within 1 inch (25 mm) of a free edge at ≥50% DAC in a
fatigue-critical joint: REJECT per engineering review.

CLAUSE 13 — LAMINATION TESTING OF BASE METAL BEFORE WELDING
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 16 — Base Metal Lamination Examination]

Base metal lamination testing (فحص طبقي للمعدن الأساسي — فحص التصفح) shall be
performed on all structural plate and tubular base material in the weld zone and
heat-affected zone (HAZ) prior to welding. This applies specifically to chord
plates of jacket legs, deck plate girders, and brace wall material in the weld
preparation zone (منطقة تحضير اللحام). Normal-beam (0°) longitudinal wave UT
using a 4–5 MHz contact transducer shall be performed in a grid pattern across
the full width of the weld preparation — a minimum of 25 mm on either side of
the weld bevel edge — at grid spacing not exceeding 50 mm × 50 mm. Calibration
for lamination scanning shall use the FBH reference block for distance setting
and the back-wall echo of the plate as the primary reference amplitude. A
complete back-wall echo loss (فقدان صدى الجدار الخلفي — total lamination)
constitutes an immediate recordable and rejectable condition. Partial back-wall
echo reduction of ≥6 dB from the nominal plate back-wall amplitude constitutes
a recordable laminar indication requiring mapping and disposition prior to
welding. Base metal lamination results shall be documented on a scaled plate
map showing all laminar indication locations, depths, and lateral extents as
measured by the 6 dB drop or edge-of-indication technique.

ACCEPTANCE: Grid scan at ≤50 mm × 50 mm spacing in weld zone. Back-wall echo
reduction <6 dB from nominal: ACCEPT (no lamination affecting weld quality).
Isolated laminations >25 mm from weld fusion line: ACCEPT with record.
Laminations fully outside the weld HAZ (>25 mm clear): ACCEPT.

REJECTION: Complete back-wall echo loss at any point in the weld preparation
zone: REJECT — material disposition required before welding. Reduction ≥6 dB
over an area ≥25 mm in any dimension within the weld zone: REJECT. Lamination
extending to a plate edge (exposed edge delamination) in a load-bearing joint:
REJECT unconditionally.

CLAUSE 14 — MAGNETIC PARTICLE EXAMINATION (MT) REQUIREMENTS FOR OFFSHORE WELDS
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 17 — Magnetic Particle Examination]

Magnetic particle examination (فحص الجسيمات المغناطيسية) shall be performed on
all accessible surfaces of primary structural welds and heat-affected zones on
offshore structures per API RP 2X Section 17. MT is mandatory for: all full-
penetration tubular joint welds (T, Y, K, X) after final post-weld grinding and
before coating; all plate butt welds in primary deck plate and jacket frame members;
and all repair welds after completion of repair excavation and re-welding. Both
pre-weld (PWMT — فحص ما قبل اللحام) and post-weld (SWMT — فحص ما بعد اللحام)
MT may be required by the engineering inspection and test plan; pre-weld MT is
performed on the prepared bevel surfaces to detect laminar and linear defects
that would be entrapped if welding proceeds. Post-weld MT shall be performed after
post-weld heat treatment (PWHT) where specified, never before. The magnetizing
technique shall be selected based on the orientation of potential defects: yoke
technique (طريقة النير) for surface and near-surface indications with yoke leg
spacing 75–200 mm; prod technique (طريقة الأقطاب) for large flat surfaces with
prod spacing 75–200 mm. Two magnetization directions at approximately 90° to each
other shall be applied at each test location to ensure detection of all defect
orientations. Prod technique is prohibited on austenitic stainless components or
where arc strikes from prod contact are unacceptable per the Owner's specification.

ACCEPTANCE: MT performed post-welding and post-grinding on all primary tubular
and butt welds. Two magnetization directions at ≥90° apart at each location.
Yoke leg spacing 75–200 mm. Prod spacing 75–200 mm. Pre-weld MT on bevel where
specified in ITP. Post-PWHT timing observed.

REJECTION: MT performed before post-weld grinding is complete. Single
magnetization direction only. Yoke or prod spacing outside 75–200 mm range.
Prod technique on stainless or where arc burns are unacceptable. MT performed
before PWHT where PWHT is specified.

CLAUSE 15 — WET FLUORESCENT MT (WFMT) FOR OFFSHORE NODES
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 18 — Wet Fluorescent Magnetic Particle Technique]

Wet fluorescent magnetic particle examination (الفحص بالجسيمات المغناطيسية
المتألقة الرطبة — WFMT) shall be used in preference to dry or wet visible
particle techniques on all node welds (لحامات العقد) and all tubular joint
welds where service criticality is classified as primary structural (primary
cyclic fatigue-loaded connections) by the structural engineer. WFMT provides
superior sensitivity for fine, tight surface-breaking cracks in the HAZ of
high-restraint offshore nodal welds compared to visible MT methods. Fluorescent
particle concentration shall be between 0.1 mL and 0.4 mL settled particles
per 100 mL of vehicle (bath concentration — تركيز المحلول) as measured by pear-
shaped centrifuge tube, checked at the start of each examination session. Black
light (UV-A lamp — مصباح الأشعة فوق البنفسجية) intensity at the examination
surface shall be ≥1000 µW/cm² measured with a calibrated UV meter. White light
at the examination surface shall be ≤2 foot-candles (≤21.5 lux) to prevent
washing out fluorescent indication visibility. All inspectors performing WFMT
shall allow a minimum dark adaptation period of 1 minute before beginning
examination under UV light. Yoke (AC) technique is preferred for WFMT on node
welds; prod technique is prohibited on node outer surfaces due to the risk of
arc burns in fatigue-critical zones. Examination coverage shall be 100% of the
weld cap surface and minimum 25 mm into adjacent base metal on each side.

ACCEPTANCE: Fluorescent bath concentration 0.1–0.4 mL/100 mL. UV intensity
≥1000 µW/cm² at examination surface. Ambient white light ≤2 foot-candles
(≤21.5 lux). Dark adaptation ≥1 minute before UV examination. AC yoke for
node WFMT. 100% weld cap + 25 mm HAZ coverage each side.

REJECTION: Bath concentration outside 0.1–0.4 mL/100 mL range. UV intensity
<1000 µW/cm². Ambient white light >2 foot-candles during WFMT. Prod technique
on node surface. WFMT performed without calibrated UV meter measurement before
session. Dark adaptation time not observed.

CLAUSE 16 — MT ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA — LINEAR VS. NON-LINEAR INDICATIONS
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 19 — MT Acceptance Criteria]

MT acceptance criteria (معايير القبول للفحص بالالتهاب المغناطيسي) for offshore
structural welds are based on indication morphology (linear vs. rounded/non-linear)
and indication length. A linear indication (مؤشر خطي) is defined as any MT
indication with a length-to-width ratio greater than 3:1; linear indications
are treated as cracks, cold laps, or fusion defects until proven otherwise by
further investigation. A non-linear (rounded) indication (مؤشر دائري / غير خطي)
is any indication with a length-to-width ratio ≤ 3:1. All linear indications
regardless of length detected in any primary structural weld or HAZ are REJECTED.
Non-linear indications: individual rounded indications with maximum dimension
≤2 mm (5/64 inch) in any direction are acceptable without repair (ACCEPT).
Non-linear indications >2 mm but ≤5 mm in maximum dimension are recordable and
subject to Level III disposition; accept/reject decision is based on location
(surface vs. sub-surface), proximity to weld toe (critical fatigue zone), and
indication density. For node welds and tubular T, Y, K joints specifically:
indications of any type within 3 mm of the weld toe or cap-to-plate interface
are treated as REJECTION candidates pending Level III engineering review. Aligned
non-linear indications (aggregate length of aligned indications in a line):
if three or more non-linear indications are aligned within a zone of 50 mm in
length, the aggregate is evaluated as a linear indication and rejected.

ACCEPTANCE: Non-linear indication maximum dimension ≤2 mm: ACCEPT.
No linear indications present. Three or fewer non-linear indications in any
50 mm zone, non-aligned: ACCEPT with record if each ≤2 mm.

REJECTION: Any linear indication (L/W > 3:1) regardless of length: REJECT
immediately — excavation and repair required (حفر وإصلاح فوري). Non-linear
indication >5 mm: REJECT. Three or more aligned non-linear indications in
50 mm zone: REJECT (treated as linear). Any indication within 3 mm of weld
toe in tubular primary joint: REJECT pending Level III engineering review.

CLAUSE 17 — UT TECHNICIAN QUALIFICATION — API 2X CERTIFICATION VS. ASNT
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 5.3 — Certification Pathways]

API RP 2X provides two recognized qualification and certification pathways for
UT technicians performing offshore structural examination. The primary pathway
is the API 2X examination program (برنامج اختبار API 2X) consisting of a written
examination and a practical performance demonstration administered by a third-party
body accredited by the Owner or certifying authority. The secondary pathway
accepts technicians certified under ASNT SNT-TC-1A or ASNT CP-189 (Level II or
Level III) provided the employer Written Practice covers the specific techniques,
frequencies, and joint geometries applicable to offshore structural fabrication
and the technician has documented hours in offshore UT techniques. Mutual
recognition of CSWIP 3.4 (PCN Level 2 UT — Structural Welds) or EN ISO 9712
Level 2 certifications may be accepted by the Owner as equivalent, subject to
Owner's written confirmation before examination commencement. Recertification
cycle (دورة إعادة التصنيف) for API 2X qualified technicians is every 3 years, with
annual vision verification (near vision Jaeger J-2 or equivalent, and color
distinction). Vision examination records must be current (within 12 months) at
the time of examination. A technician whose certification has lapsed for >6 months
must re-sit both written and practical examinations in full before reinstatement.

ACCEPTANCE: API 2X direct certification OR ASNT SNT-TC-1A/CP-189 Level II with
Owner-accepted Written Practice covering offshore structural techniques. Annual
vision check current. Certification valid (within 3-year cycle). CSWIP 3.4 or
EN ISO 9712 Level 2 accepted with Owner written approval.

REJECTION: Certification lapsed >3 years without renewal examination. Vision
examination expired >12 months. Technician using certification from unrelated
NDT sector (e.g., aerospace UT only) without demonstrated structural weld
offshore experience. ASNT certification without Written Practice covering offshore
structural geometry and techniques.

CLAUSE 18 — WRITTEN EXAMINATION AND PRACTICAL TEST REQUIREMENTS
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 5.4 — Examination Requirements]

The API RP 2X Level II qualification examination (اختبار التأهيل للمستوى الثاني)
consists of two components: a written examination (الاختبار الكتابي) and a
practical performance test (الاختبار العملي الميداني). The written examination
covers: UT theory and principles (مبادئ الموجات فوق الصوتية), equipment
calibration procedures, DAC construction and use, scanning patterns for plate and
tubular geometry, recording and acceptance criteria per API RP 2X, weld defect
recognition and characterization, and MT principles and acceptance criteria.
The passing score for the written examination is 70% minimum overall with no
single subject area falling below 50% correct answers. The practical test requires
the candidate to: calibrate the UT instrument using a provided reference block
for the applicable weld thickness range; perform a complete scan of a test plate
or tubular weld specimen containing natural or machined defects; correctly identify
and record all seeded defects at or above the specified recording threshold; and
correctly accept or reject all indications per API 2X Table 15.1. The practical
test is evaluated by a Level III examiner (محكّم المستوى الثالث) and must achieve
≥70% correct identification of seeded indications and 100% detection of any
indication with amplitude ≥100% DAC. A candidate who fails either the written or
practical component may re-attempt once after a minimum 30-day waiting period with
documented remedial training (تدريب تعويضي موثق).

ACCEPTANCE: Written examination ≥70% overall, ≥50% per subject. Practical test:
≥70% correct indication identification AND 100% detection of all indications
≥100% DAC. Level III examiner administers and witnesses practical test.
Documented remedial training before re-attempt.

REJECTION: Written score <70% overall OR any subject <50%: FAIL — 30-day waiting
period required. Practical test: missed any indication ≥100% DAC: FAIL
unconditionally regardless of overall practical score. More than two total
examination attempts without Level III documented remedial program review: REJECT
— referred to certifying authority for case review.

CLAUSE 19 — RE-EXAMINATION AFTER REPAIR WELDING
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 20 — Re-examination After Repair]

All repair welds (لحامات الإصلاح) made on offshore structural members following
rejection of original UT or MT examination must be re-examined by the full
applicable NDT procedure after repair completion and any required post-weld heat
treatment. Re-examination scope shall cover the full original examination area
plus a minimum extension of 50 mm beyond each end of the recorded indication
location to ensure that the defect has been fully removed and that no adjacent
indications were introduced by the repair welding process. Before the repair
weld UT/MT is performed, a pre-repair UT scan shall document the defect
removal: for volumetric defects, the excavation cavity shall be UT scanned to
confirm no residual defect metal remains; for surface defects removed by grinding,
WFMT shall confirm complete removal before re-welding commences. The repair weld
procedure (WPS for repair — إجراء لحام الإصلاح) must be separately qualified or
the production WPS demonstrated to be applicable to the repair configuration;
blind-side repairs and back-gouge repairs have separate qualification requirements.
Second repair attempts (second excavation of the same location — الحفر الثاني في
نفس الموقع) require documented Owner engineering authorization. Third and
subsequent repair attempts at the same location require Owner engineering Director
approval and a formal root-cause analysis of the original weld quality failure.

ACCEPTANCE: Re-examination covers original area + 50 mm extension each end.
Pre-repair cavity scan confirms defect removal. WFMT confirms clean surface
before re-weld. Re-examination to full procedure per applicable API 2X clause.
First repair re-exam authorized by QC department.

REJECTION: Re-examination scope limited to exact indication length only without
50 mm extension: NON-CONFORMING. Repair weld examined before PWHT where PWHT
is required by WPS. Third+ repair attempted without Owner engineering Director
written authorization. Any repair re-examination performed by a lower Level
technician than who performed the original examination.

CLAUSE 20 — DOCUMENTATION AND FINAL UT/MT WELD MAPPING
[Section: API RP 2X, Section 21 — Documentation Requirements]

Final documentation (التوثيق النهائي) of all UT and MT examinations on offshore
structural fabrication shall constitute a complete and traceable inspection
record (سجل الفحص الكامل والمتتبع) that supports the structural integrity basis
for the offshore unit over its design life. Each weld examined shall have a
completed examination report form containing: weld ID, structure member
designation, weld type and joint configuration, material specification and
heat number, weld thickness, welding procedure reference (WPS number),
inspection and test plan reference, NDT procedure number and revision,
equipment serial numbers and calibration certificate references, transducer
serial numbers and frequency, calibration block ID, all indication records
per Clause 11 (recording threshold), final accept/reject disposition, technician
name and certification number, Level III reviewer signature, examination date,
and any repair weld references. A scaled weld map (خريطة اللحام المقياسية)
shall be produced for each complete node or tubular assembly showing weld seam
locations, NDT method applied zone by zone, all recorded indications (location
and amplitude), repairs, and final status. The final documentation package shall
be compiled into a dossier (ملف التفتيش) for each structural assembly and
submitted to the Owner's authorized representative for final review and acceptance
before the assembly is released for load-out. All records shall be retained for
the full design life of the structure plus a minimum of 5 years (or as specified
by Owner, whichever is longer), in both hardcopy and electronic format. Electronic
records shall be stored in a document management system (DMS — نظام إدارة
الوثائق) with access controls, audit trail, and backup to prevent loss.

ACCEPTANCE: Complete examination report per all required fields above. Scaled
weld map for each node and tubular assembly. Final dossier accepted by Owner's
authorized representative in writing. Records retained for design life + 5 years
minimum in both hardcopy and electronic formats with DMS backup.

REJECTION: Examination report with missing mandatory fields (weld ID, tech cert
number, equipment serial, calibration ref, disposition): NON-CONFORMING — record
not valid for release. Weld released for load-out without Owner written acceptance
of documentation package. Records stored only in hardcopy without electronic
backup. Electronic files stored without access control or audit trail in a
DMS-equivalent system.
