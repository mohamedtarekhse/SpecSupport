CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 400.1 — SCOPE OF APPLICATION (نطاق التطبيق)

This code covers design, materials, fabrication, installation, inspection, testing, operation,
and maintenance of piping systems used to transport liquids — including crude oil (خام البترول),
petroleum products (منتجات بترولية), liquid CO2, anhydrous ammonia, and liquid slurries
(سوائل عالقة) between production facilities, tank farms, refineries, marine terminals, and
delivery points. The code applies to all pressure-containing components within the pipeline
system boundary, including pipe, fittings, valves, flanges, and appurtenances.
B31.4 does NOT cover gas pipelines — those fall under B31.8; and does NOT cover pressure
vessels governed by ASME Section VIII. Offshore liquid pipelines may use B31.4 or
B31.8S as applicable; the code boundary ends at the first block valve of a platform or
facility. The inspector (المفتش) must confirm the applicable code scope before beginning
any examination, as acceptance criteria differ fundamentally from B31.3 process piping.

ACCEPTANCE: System fluid confirmed as liquid, CO2 (liquid phase), slurry, or anhydrous
ammonia. Operating pressure and temperature within published design envelope.
Pipeline geometry (buried/aboveground) documented in the engineering data sheet (ورقة
البيانات الهندسية).

REJECTION: Application of B31.4 criteria to a gas pipeline, or use of B31.3 weld
acceptance on a liquid trunk line — non-conformance (عدم مطابقة) to be raised immediately.
Any pipeline system transporting fluids outside scope without written engineering deviation.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 400.2 — DESIGN INTENT AND MAOP DEFINITION
(الحد الأقصى لضغط التشغيل المسموح به)

MAOP (Maximum Allowable Operating Pressure — أقصى ضغط تشغيل مسموح) is the maximum
internal pressure at which a pipeline or pipeline segment may be continuously operated,
as established by the design calculations, material properties, location class, and
hydrostatic test pressure. MAOP is established for each segment individually and must
account for the highest elevation point (lowest pressure) and lowest elevation point
(highest static head). The pipeline operator must document the MAOP for every segment
in the pipeline operations manual (دليل التشغيل) and post it at block valve stations.
MAOP may never be exceeded during normal operations, pigging (تشغيل الخنزير),
or pressure surge events without a surge analysis and written authorization.
All operating pressure gauges (مقاييس الضغط) must be calibrated within 6 months and must
display MAOP as a marked reference line or tagged value.

ACCEPTANCE: Recorded operating pressure ≤ MAOP at all times. MAOP documented in
engineering file with traceability to design calculation and hydrostatic test. Gauge
calibration current (within 6 months).

REJECTION: Any recorded operating pressure exceeding MAOP, even momentarily, without
a documented surge analysis. MAOP not traceable to design calculation or hydrostatic
test record. Operating gauges uncalibrated or untagged.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 401.2.2 — MAOP DESIGN FORMULA (معادلة الضغط التصميمي)

The fundamental hoop stress formula for determining internal design pressure is:
    P = (2 × S × t) / D × F × E × T
Where:
  P = Internal design pressure (psi or MPa) (ضغط التصميم الداخلي)
  S = Specified Minimum Yield Strength — SMYS (أدنى قوة خضوع محددة) in psi or MPa
  t = Nominal wall thickness (السُّمك الاسمي للجدار) in inches or mm
  D = Outside diameter of pipe (القطر الخارجي للأنبوب) in inches or mm
  F = Design factor (معامل التصميم) based on location class — see § 401.2.2 Table
  E = Longitudinal joint factor (معامل الوصلة الطولية) — 1.0 for seamless and SAW
  T = Temperature derating factor — 1.0 for temperatures ≤ 250°F (121°C)

The inspector must verify that each term used in the pressure rating calculation is
supported by mill certificates (شهادات المصنع) and the pipeline design basis document.
Any substitution of a lower-grade pipe requires full recalculation of MAOP for the segment.
Never allow field personnel to operate above a pressure that exceeds the calculated P for
the installed pipe grade, wall thickness, and location class design factor F.

ACCEPTANCE: Calculated P ≥ MAOP for the segment. All variables (S, t, D, F, E, T)
confirmed from certified mill test reports (MTRs) and design document. E = 1.0 for
seamless (مسلوب) or SAW pipe; E = 0.80 for furnace butt weld pipe.

REJECTION: Operating MAOP exceeds calculated P. Mill certificate (شهادة المطحنة) not
available for installed pipe — SMYS cannot be assumed. Temperature derating factor
not applied when operating above 250°F. Longitudinal joint factor not verified for
electric resistance welded (ERW — ملحوم بالمقاومة الكهربائية) seam pipe.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 401.2.2 TABLE — LOCATION CLASS AND DESIGN FACTOR F
(فئة الموقع ومعامل التصميم)

B31.4 does not divide pipelines into multiple location classes the same way B31.8 does
for gas — for liquid pipelines the standard design factor F is 0.72 for all buried
cross-country liquid pipelines not otherwise restricted. However, the design factor
must be reduced to F = 0.60 for pipe installed within highway or railroad crossings
(تقاطعات الطرق والسكك الحديدية), navigable water crossings (عبور المجاري المائية), and
within compressor or pump station boundaries. Where a pipeline traverses an area of
high consequence (HCA — منطقة ذات عواقب وخيمة) such as populated areas, F shall not
exceed 0.50 unless a higher factor is justified by integrity assessment and approved
by the operator's integrity management program. The inspector must confirm the location
class and F used in the design basis document against the actual field routing survey.
Any routing change that places a segment in a more sensitive area must trigger a
re-evaluation of the applicable design factor before commissioning.

ACCEPTANCE: F = 0.72 for standard buried cross-country liquid pipeline.
F = 0.60 at road/rail/water crossings and within pump station limits.
F ≤ 0.50 in documented HCA zones with integrity program authorization.

REJECTION: F = 0.72 applied at a highway crossing or navigable water crossing — REJECT.
F not reduced when routing change places pipeline in a more sensitive zone — REJECT.
Design factor not documented in engineering file — pipeline must not be commissioned.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 401.2.1 — MINIMUM WALL THICKNESS FORMULA
(الحد الأدنى لسُمك الجدار)

The minimum required wall thickness (t_min) for straight pipe under internal pressure is
derived by rearranging the MAOP formula:
    t_min = (P × D) / (2 × S × F × E × T)
After calculating t_min, a manufacturing tolerance (تفاوت التصنيع) of −12.5% on wall
thickness must be added to arrive at the nominal ordered thickness (السُّمك الاسمي
للطلب). Therefore: t_nominal ≥ t_min / (1 − 0.125) = t_min / 0.875.
The inspector must verify mill-supplied wall thickness readings from ultrasonic
measurements (UT — قياسات الموجات فوق الصوتية) or from mill hydrostatic test records.
If in-service corrosion has reduced wall thickness below t_min at any point, the segment
must be evaluated using the applicable fitness-for-service (FFS — الصلاحية للخدمة)
methodology before continued operation. A corrosion allowance is not built into B31.4
formulas — it must be added separately by the operator's design specification.

ACCEPTANCE: Measured wall thickness ≥ t_min calculated for the governing MAOP, pipe OD,
SMYS, and F. Mill tolerance not to reduce any point below 87.5% of nominal wall.
Minimum ordered nominal wall ≥ t_min / 0.875.

REJECTION: Any measured wall thickness (field UT reading) < t_min — segment must be
de-rated or repaired before return to service. Mill cert showing wall thickness below
87.5% of nominal — pipe rejected and segregated. Corrosion-reduced wall at or below
t_min without FFS evaluation — immediate shutdown trigger.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 406.2 — FIELD BENDS — MINIMUM BENDING RADIUS
(أنصاف أقطار الانحناء في الحقل)

Cold field bends (الانحناءات الميدانية الباردة) are permitted on liquid pipelines provided
the bending radius and out-of-roundness after bending remain within code limits.
The minimum bending radius shall not be less than 18 pipe diameters (18D) for pipe
wall thicknesses ≤ 0.375 in (9.5 mm), and not less than 21D for wall thicknesses
> 0.375 in (9.5 mm), unless a lesser radius is qualified by a written bending procedure
that includes inspection of the bend for cracks (تشققات), wrinkles (تجاعيد), and
out-of-roundness. Out-of-roundness (تغيير الشكل الدائري) at any cross-section of the
bend must not exceed 2.5% of the nominal pipe OD — measured as:
    % OOR = (D_max − D_min) / D_nominal × 100
The inspector must measure OOR with a pi-tape or inside diameter template (قالب القطر
الداخلي) at the mid-point and both tangent points of each field bend. Any visible wrinkle
on the intrados (الجانب الداخلي) of the bend is cause for rejection regardless of OOR value.

ACCEPTANCE: Bending radius ≥ 18D for t ≤ 0.375 in; ≥ 21D for t > 0.375 in.
Out-of-roundness ≤ 2.5% OD after bending. No visible wrinkles on intrados.
Bend free of cracks confirmed by visual and magnetic particle (MPI — الجسيمات المغناطيسية)
or dye penetrant (PT — الاختبار بالصبغة النافذة) examination.

REJECTION: Bending radius < 18D (light wall) or < 21D (heavy wall) without qualified
procedure — REJECT bend, excavate and replace. OOR > 2.5% — reject bend, assess
for buckling risk. Any visible wrinkle on intrados — mandatory rejection and replacement.
Cracks detected by MPI or PT — bend rejected and cut out.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 434.1 — BUTT WELD JOINT REQUIREMENTS (متطلبات اللحام بالربط الكامل)

All butt welds (لحامات الربط الكامل) on liquid pipeline mainline (الخط الرئيسي) shall be
full-penetration groove welds (لحامات كاملة النفاذ) made in accordance with a qualified
Welding Procedure Specification (WPS — مواصفة إجراء اللحام) and by welders holding
current Procedure Qualification Records (PQR) and Welder Performance Qualifications
(WPQ — توصيف مؤهلات اللحام). End preparation (إعداد الطرف) shall be as specified in the
WPS — typically 30° ± 5° bevel with a root face of 1/16 ± 1/32 in (1.6 ± 0.8 mm).
Pipe ends must be cleaned (تنظيف الأطراف) of mill scale, rust, moisture, and oil to a
minimum of 1 inch (25 mm) back from the weld bevel before welding commences.
External lineup clamps (مشابك المحاذاة الخارجية) shall not be released until a minimum
of the first two passes (root and hot pass — طبقة الجذر والطبقة الحارة) are complete.
High-low misalignment (عدم المحاذاة العالي المنخفض) at the joint shall not exceed 1/8 in
(3.2 mm) before welding, measured on the outside diameter.

ACCEPTANCE: Full-penetration butt weld made per qualified WPS. End preparation as per
WPS ± stated tolerances. High-low misalignment ≤ 1/8 in (3.2 mm). External lineup
clamp not removed before root + hot pass complete. Pipe ends clean ≥ 1 in (25 mm)
from bevel. Preheat applied per WPS (minimum temperature verified by contact
thermometer or temperature-indicating crayons — أقلام قياس الحرارة).

REJECTION: Butt weld made without qualified WPS or by unqualified welder — REJECT weld,
cut out. High-low > 3.2 mm before welding — re-align before proceeding.
Lineup clamp removed before root + hot pass — weld must be cut out and re-welded.
Preheat not applied when required by WPS — weld rejected and re-made.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 APPENDIX B — WELD ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA (RT/UT)
(معايير قبول اللحامات — الأشعة والموجات فوق الصوتية)

B31.4 Appendix B establishes the mandatory acceptance/rejection criteria for radiographic
(RT — الأشعة السينية) and ultrasonic (UT — الموجات فوق الصوتية) examination of pipeline
girth welds (اللحامات الدائرية). These criteria are more stringent than B31.3 for some
defect types due to fatigue and cyclic pressure loading on liquid pipelines.
Radiographic film (فيلم الأشعة السينية) or digital detector must be evaluated by a
Level II or III RT interpreter qualified to SNT-TC-1A or equivalent.
Ultrasonic examination must use a qualified procedure with demonstrated capability on
representative test blocks; automated UT (AUT) is permitted and preferred for
mechanized welding. The following are the primary RT/UT acceptance criteria per
Appendix B — each defect type is treated separately.

INCOMPLETE FUSION (اندماج ناقص) / INCOMPLETE PENETRATION (نفاذ ناقص):
ACCEPTANCE: None permitted regardless of length. Zero tolerance.
REJECTION: Any indication of incomplete fusion or incomplete penetration — REJECT weld.

POROSITY (مسامية):
ACCEPTANCE: Individual pore ≤ 3/32 in (2.4 mm) diameter. Cluster porosity ≤ 1/2 in
(12.7 mm) in any 12 in (300 mm) weld length. Total porosity area ≤ 2% of weld image
area in any 6 in (150 mm) length of radiograph.
REJECTION: Any pore > 3/32 in (2.4 mm). Cluster porosity > 12.7 mm aggregate extent.
Total pore area > 2% in any 6 in (150 mm) length.

SLAG INCLUSIONS / ELONGATED INDICATIONS (إدراجات الخبث):
ACCEPTANCE: Individual slag length ≤ 2 in (50 mm) in any 12 in (300 mm) weld length.
Width ≤ 1/16 in (1.6 mm). Aggregate slag ≤ 2 in (50 mm) in any 12 in weld length.
REJECTION: Individual slag > 50 mm length or > 1.6 mm width. Aggregate slag > 50 mm
in any 12 in length.

CRACKS (تشققات): ACCEPTANCE: None — zero tolerance regardless of length, orientation,
or location. REJECTION: Any crack indication confirmed by RT or UT — mandatory cut-out.

UNDERCUTTING (الحفر الجانبي):
ACCEPTANCE: Maximum depth 1/32 in (0.8 mm). Length unlimited if within depth limit.
REJECTION: Undercut depth > 0.8 mm at any location on girth weld — weld must be
repaired by grinding and re-welding or by deposition of additional weld passes.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 434.8 — WELDING PROCEDURE AND WELDER QUALIFICATION
(التأهيل الإجرائي للحام وتأهيل اللحام)

All welding on B31.4 liquid pipeline systems shall be performed to a WPS qualified in
accordance with ASME Section IX (الجزء التاسع من ASME) or API 1104, as specified by the
operator's engineering standard. Qualification of the WPS requires a Procedure
Qualification Record (PQR — سجل تأهيل الإجراء) supported by mechanical test results
including tensile (شد), guided bend (ثني موجه), and nick break (كسر النيك) tests.
Welder qualification (تأهيل اللحام) must be documented per the same standard and must
include the process (SMAW, GTAW, SAW, GMAW), position, pipe OD group, and wall
thickness group. Welder qualification expires if the welder has not welded with the
qualified process for more than 6 months — or if specific cause for questioning
competency arises (e.g., failure of a production weld on RT). The inspector must
check the welder's stamp (ختم اللحام) or ID number on the weld map (خريطة اللحامات)
and cross-reference against the qualification log before allowing any welder to
proceed on mainline tie-in welds (لحامات الربط).

ACCEPTANCE: Valid WPS and PQR on file covering process, position, pipe OD, and wall
thickness. Welder's WPQ current (welded within 6 months on qualified process).
Welder ID traceable on weld log to specific joint. Preheat, interpass temperature,
and PWHT (if required by WPS) verified and recorded.

REJECTION: WPS not qualified or PQR missing mechanical test data — no welding
permitted. Welder qualification expired (>6 months without qualified process use) —
welder must re-qualify before working on mainline. Welder ID not recorded on weld
log — weld must be treated as unqualified and subjected to 100% RT/UT.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 437 — BRANCH CONNECTION REQUIREMENTS (متطلبات توصيلات التفريع)

Branch connections (توصيلات التفريع) on liquid pipelines must be designed and fabricated
to maintain the pressure integrity of the header pipe (أنبوب الرأسي الرئيسي) without
exceeding the allowable hoop stress. Unreinforced fabricated tees (تيهات غير معززة)
are permitted only where the calculated area replacement (مساحة الاستبدال) requirements
of B31.4 § 437 are satisfied — i.e., the metal removed from the header must be
replaced by reinforcing pad (لوحة التعزيز), the branch wall, or branch fitting excess metal.
Hot tapping (الحفر الساخن) on in-service pressurized pipelines is permitted subject to
a written hot tap procedure approved by the operator, pressure and temperature within
the fitting manufacturer's rating, and the absence of flammable vapor accumulation.
Hot tap fittings (تركيبات الحفر الساخن) must be full-encirclement type when installed on
a pipe with wall loss > 10% of nominal. Branch-to-header weld must achieve full
penetration and be examined by RT or UT to the same acceptance criteria as mainline
girth welds. Set-on (وضع على) branch connections are not permitted on pipelines
operating above 20% SMYS hoop stress — use integrally reinforced fittings.

ACCEPTANCE: Area replacement calculation satisfied per § 437 for all unreinforced
fabricated branches. Hot tap procedure written, approved, and fitting rated for
operating conditions. Full-penetration weld on branch-to-header confirmed by RT/UT.
Reinforcing pad vent hole (فتحة تنفيس لوحة التعزيز) provided and tested with soap
solution at 15 psi (103 kPa) after welding to confirm pad seal.

REJECTION: Area replacement not satisfied for fabricated branch — REJECT, redesign with
rated tee fitting. Hot tap attempted without written procedure — stop work immediately.
Reinforcing pad installed without vent hole — reject until drilled and tested.
Set-on branch on pipe with hoop stress > 20% SMYS — reject and replace with
integrally reinforced fitting.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 437.4 — HYDROSTATIC TEST PRESSURE REQUIREMENT
(متطلبات اختبار الضغط الهيدروستاتيكي)

Before placing a new or relocated pipeline segment into liquid service, a hydrostatic
pressure test (اختبار الضغط الهيدروستاتيكي) must be conducted using water (ماء) as the
test medium unless water is not available or would create a hazard, in which case
another incompressible liquid approved by the operator may be substituted.
The minimum hydrostatic test pressure at any point along the segment shall be not less
than 1.25 × MAOP for buried pipelines and 1.10 × MAOP for aboveground pipelines
(خطوط فوق السطح). The test pressure must be maintained at the lowest elevation of the
test segment — static head contribution at higher elevation points must be calculated
and subtracted to ensure the highest elevation does not see excessive pressure.
Test pressure must be measured with calibrated gauges (دقة المقياس المعايَر) at the
inlet and at the highest elevation point of the test section. All air (هواء) must be
vented from high points (نقاط التهوية) before pressurizing to test pressure to avoid
a pneumatic component in what is intended as a hydrostatic test.

ACCEPTANCE: Test pressure ≥ 1.25 × MAOP for buried pipe; ≥ 1.10 × MAOP for
aboveground pipe — measured at the low-elevation end of the test section.
Test medium: water or approved incompressible liquid. Calibrated gauges at inlet
and high point. All high-point vents opened until water flows, then closed before
pressuring to test level.

REJECTION: Test pressure < 1.25 × MAOP for buried segment — test invalid, must be
repeated. Air pocket not vented before pressurization — pneumatic energy hazard,
depressurize and vent before re-testing. Test gauge calibration expired — test
data invalid, must be repeated with calibrated equipment.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 437.4.1 — HYDROSTATIC TEST HOLD TIME (وقت الإمساك بالضغط)

For buried liquid pipelines (الأنابيب المدفونة), the minimum hold time (وقت الإمساك)
at the required test pressure is 4 hours continuous. During this hold period, no
make-up water or pressure may be added — if pressure cannot be maintained without
pumping, a leak exists. For aboveground piping systems (المنظومة فوق الأرض) within
a liquid pipeline facility, the hold time may be reduced to 1 hour provided all welds
and connections are visually inspected during the hold period.
The inspector must record pressure and temperature readings (قراءات الضغط والحرارة)
at a minimum of every 30 minutes during the hold period on the test data sheet
(ورقة بيانات الاختبار). Temperature correction (تصحيح درجة الحرارة) must be applied
if ambient temperature changes by more than 5°F (2.8°C) during the test, using the
gas laws or the operator's approved correction formula to distinguish a thermal
pressure variation from a true leak. The test record must be signed by the inspector,
witnessed by the operator's representative (ممثل المشغل), and filed as a permanent
engineering record.

ACCEPTANCE: Hold time ≥ 4 hours for buried pipeline; ≥ 1 hour for aboveground facility
piping. Pressure records taken every ≤ 30 minutes with timestamps. No pressure drop
unexplained by temperature variation. Temperature recorded every 30 minutes at a
minimum of two points along the test section.

REJECTION: Hold time < 4 hours for buried pipeline — test invalid, repeat from initial
pressurization. Any unexplained pressure drop during hold period — test FAILS,
depressurize, locate and repair leak, re-test. Temperature correction not documented
when ambient change > 5°F — test record incomplete, subject to re-test requirement.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 437.4.3 — TEST PRESSURE UPPER LIMIT — 90% SMYS
(الحد الأقصى لضغط الاختبار — 90% من أدنى قوة الخضوع)

While the minimum hydrostatic test pressure is 1.25 × MAOP, the code also imposes a
ceiling: the test pressure must not cause the hoop stress in the pipe wall to exceed
90% of SMYS (أدنى قوة الخضوع المحددة) at any point along the test segment.
This upper limit protects the pipe from yielding (الخضوع البلاستيكي) during the test,
which would permanently deform the pipe wall and reduce the load-carrying capacity of
the cross-section. At the lowest elevation (highest pressure) point of the test segment,
the inspector must calculate the resulting hoop stress using:
    σ_h = (P_test × D) / (2 × t)
and confirm that σ_h ≤ 0.90 × SMYS.
If the test segment has elevation differences that cause the test pressure at the low
point to exceed the 90% SMYS limit, the test section must be split into shorter segments
(تقسيم قطاع الاختبار) so each can be tested within these bounds. Document the break
points and the maximum allowable test pressure for each sub-segment in the test plan.

ACCEPTANCE: Hoop stress at lowest elevation ≤ 90% SMYS (calculated from P_test, D, t).
Test pressure at high-elevation end ≥ 1.25 × MAOP.
Both conditions satisfied simultaneously — test plan valid.

REJECTION: Test pressure at low-elevation point causes hoop stress > 90% SMYS —
reduce test pressure, split segment, or recalculate. Segment tested as a single section
when elevation difference causes one end to violate upper or lower limit — test
plan invalid. SMYS used in calculation not verified by mill certificate — use only
certified MTR values.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 437.5 — PNEUMATIC TEST — CONDITIONS AND LIMITS
(الاختبار الهوائي — الشروط والحدود)

A pneumatic test (اختبار هوائي) using compressed gas (gas, nitrogen — نيتروجين, or air)
may only be used as an alternative to hydrostatic testing when the operator can
demonstrate that water would create a hazardous condition (e.g., process contamination,
freeze potential, or structural overload from water weight), or when the pipeline is not
capable of being adequately drained after a hydrostatic test. Pneumatic testing is
inherently more dangerous than hydrostatic testing because compressed gas stores far
more energy — the test must be conducted with all personnel and equipment cleared from
the immediate test zone (منطقة الاختبار) during initial pressurization steps.
The maximum pneumatic test pressure shall not exceed 1.1 × MAOP — this is lower than
the hydrostatic minimum of 1.25 × MAOP specifically because of the energy stored in
compressed gas. A preliminary leak test (اختبار تسرب أولي) at 25 psig (172 kPa) must
be conducted first using a soap solution (محلول الصابون) to check all joints and
connections before pressurizing to the full pneumatic test pressure.

ACCEPTANCE: Pneumatic test only used with documented justification approved by the
operator. Maximum test pressure ≤ 1.1 × MAOP. Preliminary soap-bubble leak test
at 25 psig (172 kPa) completed and passed. Personnel safety exclusion zone established
and enforced during pressurization. Test medium: dry nitrogen (preferred), instrument
air (هواء الأدوات), or natural gas with ignition source controls documented.

REJECTION: Pneumatic test used without documented justification — invalid test, must
repeat with hydrostatic test. Test pressure > 1.1 × MAOP for pneumatic test —
immediate depressurization required. Preliminary 25 psig soap test not performed —
test invalid. Personnel inside exclusion zone during pressurization — stop work,
safety violation.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 437.4.2 — LEAK TEST ACCEPTANCE (قبول اختبار التسرب)

The hydrostatic or pneumatic test passes only when there is no pressure drop throughout
the full hold period that cannot be fully accounted for by measured temperature change.
The inspector must record the initial stabilized pressure (الضغط المستقر الابتدائي)
after the thermal stabilization period — typically 1 hour after reaching test pressure
before the official hold period begins — and compare against the final pressure at end
of hold. Any pressure drop observed must be investigated: first apply temperature
correction, then if unexplained pressure drop remains, the test is a FAILURE.
All visible joints, welds, fittings, and mechanical connections within the test boundary
must be walked and visually inspected during hydrostatic hold for seepage, weeping,
or active leaks. A calibrated chart recorder (مسجل المخططات المعايَر) or data logger
is preferred over manual readings for buried pipeline tests to provide a continuous
pressure-time trace (منحنى الضغط-الزمن) as a permanent record.

ACCEPTANCE: Zero unexplained pressure drop over the full hold period (4 hours buried,
1 hour aboveground). All temperature-corrected pressure readings within ±0.1 bar
(1.45 psi) variation attributable solely to measured temperature change.
No visible leak at any joint or fitting during walk inspection.
Chart recorder or data logger trace filed as permanent test record.

REJECTION: Any unexplained pressure drop — test FAILS regardless of drop magnitude.
Visible seepage or weeping at any joint, weld, or fitting — test FAILS, repair and
retest. Test record based on manual readings only without timestamps — record
deemed incomplete for regulatory filing. Chart recorder not calibrated — data
not acceptable; test must be repeated with calibrated equipment.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 461 — CATHODIC PROTECTION REQUIREMENTS
(متطلبات الحماية الكاثودية)

All buried or submerged metallic liquid pipeline components must be provided with
cathodic protection (CP — الحماية الكاثودية) in accordance with B31.4 § 461 and the
requirements of the applicable regulatory body (NACE SP0169 / ISO 15589-1 are
the industry reference standards). CP must be installed and operating before the
pipeline is placed in service, or within 1 year of installation of bare or poorly
coated pipe. The most common CP criterion for steel pipelines is the −850 mV
polarized potential (الجهد المستقطب) measured against a copper/copper sulfate reference
electrode (Cu/CuSO₄ — مرجع النحاس/كبريتات النحاس), measured with the protective
current applied (ON potential). Where sulfate-reducing bacteria (SRB — البكتيريا
المختزلة للكبريتات) are present in the soil, a more negative criterion of −950 mV
(Cu/CuSO₄) is required. Pipe-to-soil potential measurements (قياسات جهد الأنبوب
إلى التربة) must be taken at intervals not exceeding 1 mile (1.6 km) along the pipeline
or at every test station (محطة الاختبار), whichever is more frequent.

ACCEPTANCE: Pipe-to-soil ON potential ≤ −850 mV (Cu/CuSO₄) at all measurement points.
Instantaneous-off (IR-free) potential ≤ −850 mV (Cu/CuSO₄) preferred for accurate
assessment. In SRB environments: ON potential ≤ −950 mV. Measurement interval ≤ 1 mile
(1.6 km). Annual survey (مسح سنوي) completed and documented. Rectifier (معدل التيار)
output recorded at each survey — current output and voltage documented.

REJECTION: Pipe-to-soil ON potential more positive than −850 mV at any test station —
CP system deficient, immediate rectifier adjustment or additional anode output required.
Annual survey not conducted — regulatory non-compliance. Any section of buried pipeline
without CP system or with CP disabled for > 30 days without documented engineering
exemption — high priority corrosion risk, inspect immediately.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 462 — PRE-COMMISSIONING — CLEANING AND GAUGING
(تنظيف وقياس ما قبل التشغيل)

Before a new pipeline segment is filled with product and placed in service, the line
must be mechanically cleaned and gauged to confirm clear bore (تأكيد الفتحة النظيفة)
and absence of dents, buckles, or construction debris (حطام البناء) within the pipe bore.
Cleaning is achieved by running a series of foam pigs (خنازير إسفنجية), brush pigs
(خنازير فرشاة), and gauge pigs (خنازير القياس) through the line using water, air,
or nitrogen as the driving medium. The gauge pig carries a gauge plate (لوح القياس)
sized to 95% of the nominal inside diameter (ID — القطر الداخلي الاسمي) of the pipe —
if the gauge plate returns deformed (مشوه), a dent or obstruction reduces the bore
below 95% ID and must be located and remediated before commissioning.
Pig runs must be documented on a pig run log (سجل تشغيل الخنزير) that records
launch time, arrival time, and condition of pig and gauge plate on recovery.
Cleaning runs continue until the return medium (ماء التنظيف العائد) is visually clean.

ACCEPTANCE: Gauge plate recovered undamaged — bore ≥ 95% nominal ID confirmed.
Cleaning runs continued until return medium visually free of debris, scale, and mill
varnish. Pig run log completed with timestamps, pig type, driving medium, and
gauge plate condition for each run.

REJECTION: Gauge plate returned deformed or with indentations — bore obstruction
< 95% ID detected; excavate, locate obstruction, repair, and re-run gauge pig.
Pig not recovered at receiving end — pig stuck in line; locate and retrieve before
commissioning. Return medium still carrying debris after 5 cleaning pig runs —
investigate internal contamination, increase run frequency or change pig type.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 461.1 — IN-SERVICE INSPECTION — ILI / SMART PIG INTERVALS
(الفترات الزمنية للتفتيش الداخلي — الخنزير الذكي)

B31.4 requires operators to establish and implement an Integrity Management Program
(IMP — برنامج إدارة النزاهة) that includes regular In-Line Inspection (ILI — التفتيش
الداخلي) using smart pig tools (أدوات الخنزير الذكي) for piggable pipelines in High
Consequence Areas (HCA — مناطق العواقب الوخيمة). ILI tools include Magnetic Flux
Leakage (MFL — تسرب التدفق المغناطيسي) for metal loss detection, Ultrasonic Testing
(UT — الموجات فوق الصوتية) tools for wall thickness measurement, caliper/geometry tools
(أدوات مقياس الشكل الهندسي) for dent and deformation detection, and Elastic Wave tools
for stress corrosion cracking (SCC — التشقق بالتآكل الإجهادي) assessment.
ILI re-assessment intervals must be determined by the operator's IMP based on the
predicted corrosion growth rate and time to reach 80% wall loss (الحد الأقصى لفقدان
الجدار). Typical industry practice (ASME B31.8S-based method applied to liquid lines):
maximum interval 5 years for high-consequence corrosion threats; up to 10 years
where corrosion growth rate is demonstrated to be ≤ 0.004 in/yr (0.1 mm/yr).

ACCEPTANCE: ILI tool selected is appropriate for identified threat (MFL for metal loss,
caliper for geometry). ILI run completed within required re-assessment interval.
Tool performance specification: probability of detection (POD) ≥ 90% for defects
at 10% wall loss or greater; sizing accuracy ±10% wt for depth, ±6 in (152 mm) for
length. Post-ILI analysis report issued within 120 days of pig pull.

REJECTION: ILI re-assessment interval exceeded without documented engineering justification
and pressure de-rating — operator in non-compliance with IMP. ILI tool not appropriate
for threat type — data invalid for integrity assessment. ILI report not issued within
180 days — regulatory violation in most jurisdictions. Non-piggable pipeline in an
HCA without an approved alternative assessment method — immediate integrity concern.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 461.3 — CORROSION MONITORING AND REMAINING LIFE ASSESSMENT
(مراقبة التآكل وتقييم العمر المتبقي)

In-service corrosion (التآكل أثناء الخدمة) is managed through a combination of ILI data,
direct examination (DE — الفحص المباشر), external corrosion direct assessment (ECDA —
تقييم التآكل الخارجي المباشر), internal corrosion direct assessment (ICDA — تقييم
التآكل الداخلي المباشر), and corrosion monitoring programs (برامج مراقبة التآكل).
Wall loss at any reported ILI feature must be assessed using a validated metal loss
assessment method — ASME B31G, Modified B31G, or RSTRENG — to determine whether
the feature is acceptable for continued operation at MAOP (مقبول للتشغيل المستمر).
Remaining strength factor (RSF — معامل المقاومة المتبقية) must be ≥ 1.0 for the feature
to be acceptable without pressure de-rating. Predicted time to failure (TTF — الوقت
حتى الفشل) must be calculated using the measured or assumed corrosion growth rate,
and the re-assessment interval set so that the next ILI or direct examination occurs
before 80% of wall loss threshold is reached. Corrosion inhibitor injection (حقن
مثبط التآكل) effectiveness must be documented by quarterly coupon weight loss measurements.

ACCEPTANCE: RSF ≥ 1.0 per ASME B31G or RSTRENG for all reported ILI metal loss features.
Wall loss ≤ 80% wt (maximum acceptable depth for MAOP assessment).
Corrosion coupon loss rate ≤ 0.1 mm/yr (4 mpy) confirming inhibitor effectiveness.
Re-assessment interval set before 80% wall loss threshold is predicted to be reached.

REJECTION: RSF < 1.0 for any ILI feature at MAOP — immediate pressure de-rating to
safe operating pressure or repair before return to MAOP. Wall loss > 80% wt at any
measured location — mandatory immediate repair or cut-out. Corrosion coupon loss rate
> 0.5 mm/yr (20 mpy) — inhibitor program review and upgrade required immediately.
No TTF calculation or re-assessment interval documented — IMP non-compliance.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 451.6 — REPAIR CRITERIA — DENTS AND CORROSION DEFECTS
(معايير الإصلاح — الحفر والعيوب التآكلية)

B31.4 § 451.6 defines the conditions under which a dent (حفرة — انبعاج), gouge
(خدش عميق), or corrosion defect (عيب تآكلي) requires immediate repair or removal
from service. A plain dent (حفرة بسيطة — no metal loss or stress concentration) is
acceptable in liquid service if its depth does not exceed 6% of the pipe OD — measured
as the gap between the lowest point of the dent and the original pipe contour.
However, ANY dent that contains a stress concentrator — a scratch, gouge, arc burn
(حرق القوس), or weld — regardless of depth, is subject to immediate rejection because
of fatigue crack initiation risk under cyclic pressure loading. Corrosion pitting (نقر
التآكل) or grooving assessed by ILI or direct examination is evaluated by B31G or
RSTRENG — if remaining strength factor RSF < 1.0 for MAOP, repair is mandatory.
Smooth dents on seams, girth welds, or at branch connections are automatically rejected
regardless of depth because of the stress concentration presented by the weld.

ACCEPTANCE: Plain dent (no metal loss, no stress concentrator, not on seam/girth weld)
depth ≤ 6% OD. Corrosion defect: RSF ≥ 1.0 at MAOP per B31G/RSTRENG.
Wall loss ≤ 80% wt at deepest measured point. Dent located not on or within 2D of
a girth weld or seam weld.

REJECTION: Plain dent depth > 6% OD — immediate repair. Any dent with associated gouge,
scratch, arc burn, or weld — REJECT regardless of depth; cut out or sleeve repair.
Dent at or within 2D of a girth weld or seam weld — REJECT regardless of depth.
Corrosion defect with RSF < 1.0 at MAOP — de-rate pressure or repair before
next ILI interval. Wall loss > 80% wt at any point — cut-out and replace.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 451.6.2 — SLEEVE REPAIR REQUIREMENTS
(متطلبات إصلاح الكمّ — الغلاف المحيطي الكامل)

A full-encirclement steel sleeve (كمّ فولاذي محيطي كامل) is the preferred permanent
repair method for corrosion defects, dents with stress concentrators, and cracks on
in-service liquid pipelines where cut-out and replacement is not practical.
Type A sleeve (Pressure Sleeve — كمّ الضغط) is a full-encirclement split sleeve welded
with longitudinal seam welds only — no fillet welds to the carrier pipe (الأنبوب الحامل).
Type A is used for corrosion defects only, where the carrier pipe is not leaking.
Type B sleeve (Structural/Pressure-Containing Sleeve — كمّ هيكلي) is welded to the
carrier pipe with circumferential fillet welds at both ends — this sleeve carries
pressure and provides structural support; it is used for leaking defects, cracks,
and dents with stress concentrators. The annular space (الفجوة الحلقية) between sleeve
and carrier pipe for Type A must be filled with epoxy filler (راتنج إيبوكسي) or grout
to prevent moisture entrapment. Sleeve minimum length must extend 2 inches (50 mm)
beyond each end of the defect, and sleeve wall thickness must be ≥ carrier pipe
nominal wall thickness.

ACCEPTANCE: Full-encirclement sleeve used (no clock-spring or partial-wrap for primary
pressure repair on liquid pipelines). Sleeve length ≥ defect length + 4 in (100 mm)
total overhang (2 in each side). Sleeve wall thickness ≥ carrier pipe nominal wt.
Type A: longitudinal seam welds only, annular space filled with epoxy filler.
Type B: circumferential fillet welds at both ends to carrier pipe, full penetration.
Sleeve weld examined by MT (MPI) or PT 100% after welding and before burial.

REJECTION: Partial-wrap (non-full-encirclement) repair used as pressure-containing repair
on liquid mainline — reject, require full-encirclement. Sleeve length insufficient
(< 2 in overhang at each end) — reject, extend sleeve or install longer sleeve.
Sleeve wall thickness < carrier pipe nominal wt — reject, replace with correct wall.
Type B fillet welds not achieving full fusion at carrier pipe — reject, re-weld.
No MT/PT examination of sleeve welds before burial — require excavation and examination.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 434.7 — NON-DESTRUCTIVE EXAMINATION — EXTENT AND METHODS
(الفحص غير المتلف — الحجم والطرق)

B31.4 requires radiographic (RT) or ultrasonic (UT) examination of a minimum percentage
of girth welds on new pipeline construction, with the percentage determined by the
design factor F and the construction environment.
For pipelines designed with F = 0.72 (standard cross-country): minimum 10% of girth
welds by RT or UT — selected to include the first weld made by each welder, tie-in
welds (لحامات الربط), and welds in road/water crossings.
For pipelines designed with F = 0.60 (crossings, HCA): 100% RT or UT of all girth
welds within the crossing or HCA limits. All tie-in welds (cold tie-ins — الربط البارد)
and welds on risers (ارتفاعات الأنابيب) at pump stations and terminal facilities are
100% RT or UT regardless of location class. When RT is used, minimum single-wall
single-image (SWSI — صورة أحادية جدار واحد) technique; double-wall technique permitted
only for pipe OD ≤ 3.5 in (89 mm). RT sensitivity must be verified by image quality
indicator (IQI — مؤشر جودة الصورة) — wire IQI or hole IQI as specified in the WPS.

ACCEPTANCE: Examination percentage met per design factor: ≥ 10% for F = 0.72;
100% for F = 0.60 zones and all crossings, HCA, tie-ins, and riser welds.
RT technique verified by IQI — 2-2T sensitivity achieved (wire IQI) or equivalent
hole IQI. UT procedure qualified per ASME Section V Article 4 or API 1104 Appendix A.

REJECTION: Required examination percentage not met — pipeline cannot be commissioned
until deficiency corrected. RT films without IQI image or IQI sensitivity not achieved —
film(s) invalid, re-expose. UT operator performing examination without demonstrated
procedure qualification — examination invalid, repeat by qualified operator.
Any weld at crossing or HCA not 100% examined — stop commissioning, examine and
clear before burial of crossing.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 451.4 — PIPELINE OPERATIONS — PRESSURE CONTROL AND OVERPRESSURE
PROTECTION (التحكم في الضغط والحماية من الضغط الزائد)

Liquid pipeline systems must be equipped with pressure control and relief devices
(أجهزة التحكم في الضغط والتخفيف) to prevent exceeding MAOP during normal operations
and anticipated surge events (أحداث التموج). Pressure relief valves (صمامات التخفيف)
or pressure control valves (صمامات التحكم بالضغط) must be set to activate before the
operating pressure reaches MAOP. Relief valve set pressure must not exceed MAOP.
Relief valves shall be tested annually (سنويًا) by the operator and calibrated to
confirm set point accuracy within ±2% of the set pressure. Pressure surge analysis
(تحليل ضربة الكبش — water hammer analysis) must be performed for all systems where
rapid valve closure or pump trip can generate transient overpressure. Surge pressure
(ضغط التموج) must not cause instantaneous hoop stress to exceed 110% SMYS at any
point — if it does, surge suppression systems (systems of surge relief valves, surge
tanks — صهاريج التموج) must be installed.

ACCEPTANCE: Relief valve set point ≤ MAOP. Annual test of relief valves with set point
verified within ±2% of MAOP. Surge analysis completed and surge pressure limited
to ≤ 110% SMYS instantaneous hoop stress. Relief valve flow capacity adequate to
prevent MAOP exceedance under maximum anticipated pump output.

REJECTION: Relief valve set above MAOP — immediate recalibration required before
restart. Relief valve annual test overdue — system non-compliant, test immediately.
Surge analysis not performed — operator cannot confirm system integrity during
transient events. Surge pressure exceeding 110% SMYS without suppression system
installed — immediate engineering review and corrective action.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 461.4 — PIPELINE COATINGS AND CORROSION CONTROL
(طلاءات الأنابيب والسيطرة على التآكل)

External corrosion of buried liquid pipelines is prevented by the combination of a
protective coating (طلاء واقي) and cathodic protection — the two work together and
neither alone is sufficient per B31.4 philosophy. Coating selection, application, and
inspection must follow the operator's specification and the applicable industry standard
(NACE SP0169, SP0188, or ISO 21809). Coating types commonly used on liquid pipelines
include Fusion Bonded Epoxy (FBE — الإيبوكسي المصهور), three-layer polyethylene (3LPE —
البولي إيثيلين ثلاثي الطبقات), coal tar epoxy (قطران الفحم الإيبوكسي), and field-applied
tape coatings for girth weld areas (مناطق اللحام الدائري). Holiday detection (كشف
الثقوب الصغيرة في الطلاء) must be performed on all coated pipe joints before lowering
into the trench using a DC spark holiday detector (كاشف التشويش — جهاز كشف الثقوب)
set to 100 V per mil (3937 V/mm) of coating thickness for FBE, or as specified for
other coating types. Girth weld coating must be applied and holiday-tested before
backfill (الردم). Any holiday or coating damage must be repaired before backfill.

ACCEPTANCE: Coating type and thickness conform to operator specification and applicable
standard. Holiday detection performed 100% on all joints — no holidays permitted in
final inspection. DC holiday detector voltage calibrated and set per coating type.
Girth weld field joint coating applied, cured per manufacturer's spec, and holiday-tested.
Backfill material ≤ 1/2 in (12.7 mm) particle size for 6 in (150 mm) below and above
the pipe to prevent mechanical coating damage during backfill operations.

REJECTION: Holidays detected and not repaired before lowering — reject and repair.
Holiday detector voltage incorrect for coating type — re-inspect entire coated string
with correct voltage. Field joint coating not applied before backfill — excavate,
apply, test, and then backfill. Backfill material containing rocks > 12.7 mm in pipe
zone — remove and replace with specified bedding material.

CLAUSE REF: B31.4 § 430 — RECORDS AND DOCUMENTATION — AS-BUILT REQUIREMENTS
(السجلات والتوثيق — متطلبات الرسومات التنفيذية)

B31.4 § 430 requires the pipeline operator to maintain a complete set of records for
the life of the pipeline (طوال عمر الأنبوب). As-built drawings (الرسومات التنفيذية)
must accurately show pipeline route (مسار خط الأنابيب), depth of cover (عمق الغطاء)
at all road, water, and obstacle crossings, wall thickness and grade for each pipe
segment, valve locations (مواقع الصمامات), and cathodic protection system components.
The pipeline materials record must include mill test reports (شهادات المختبر) for all
pipe, fittings, and valves showing material grade, heat number (رقم الصهر), and
mechanical properties. Hydrostatic test records (سجلات اختبار الضغط) must be retained
permanently — they are the legal basis for MAOP establishment. Weld inspection records
(سجلات فحص اللحامات) including all RT films or digital UT data must be retained
for the life of the pipeline. ILI reports and corrosion assessment records must be
retained for a minimum of 10 years or until superseded by a subsequent ILI run,
whichever is longer.

ACCEPTANCE: As-built drawings updated within 90 days of construction completion and
filed in the operator's document control system (نظام التحكم في الوثائق). MTRs
available for all installed materials traceable to heat number. Hydrostatic test records
retained permanently. RT films or UT digital records retained for pipeline life. ILI
reports retained ≥ 10 years. Records available for regulatory inspection (تفتيش تنظيمي)
on request within 5 business days.

REJECTION: As-built drawings not updated or not traceable to installed configuration —
non-conformance requiring immediate correction. MTRs missing for installed pipe or
fittings — material SMYS cannot be verified; MAOP must be assumed based on lowest
published SMYS or pipe de-rated. Hydrostatic test records missing — MAOP cannot be
established from test; segment must be re-tested before continued operation at
current MAOP. ILI reports not retained per minimum period — regulatory non-compliance.
