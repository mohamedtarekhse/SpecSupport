[STANDARD: API Spec 4F / API RP 4G]
[TITLE: Specification for Drilling and Well Servicing Structures & Recommended Practice for Operation, Inspection, Maintenance, and Repair of Drilling and Well Servicing Structures]
[EDITION: API Spec 4F 4th/5th Edition / API RP 4G 4th Edition]
[ARTICLE: Full Scope — Masts, Derricks, Substructures, Crown Assemblies & In-Service Inspection Categories I-IV]

---
CLAUSE: API 4F Section 6 / API RP 4G Clause 8.1 — Mast Leg Straightness and Alignment Tolerances
EXPLANATION: Primary mast legs (أرجل الصاري) and derrick corner columns carry the combined compressive static hook load, pipe setback weight, and wind overturning forces. Any lateral bow, sweep, or dent induces severe secondary bending moments ($P-\delta$ effect) that dramatically reduce column buckling resistance. The straightness of mast legs must be measured between panel points (unsupported panel length L) using a taut piano wire or precision laser alignment system.
ACCEPTANCE: Maximum allowable lateral deviation (bow or sweep) from a straight line between adjacent panel points shall not exceed L / 1000, with an absolute maximum deviation not exceeding 3.2 mm (1/8 in.) for any single leg panel.
REJECTION: Any mast leg section exhibiting a lateral sweep or permanent deflection exceeding L / 1000 or greater than 3.2 mm (1/8 in.) is strictly REJECTED. The structure must be condemned from drilling operations until an OEM-engineered repair sleeve is welded per AWS D1.1 or the section is replaced.

CLAUSE: API 4F Section 6 / API RP 4G Clause 8.2 — Structural Girt and Diagonal Brace Straightness Limits
EXPLANATION: Girts and diagonal braces (الجسور والأذرع القطرية) prevent lateral-torsional buckling of the primary mast legs and transmit horizontal wind shear and dynamic seismic forces to the substructure. Bends in diagonal braces are commonly caused by drill collar handling impacts or transport collisions. Straightness must be checked across the full node-to-node length between weld joints.
ACCEPTANCE: Maximum allowable out-of-straightness for girts and diagonal braces shall not exceed L / 500, with an absolute maximum deviation of 6.4 mm (1/4 in.).
REJECTION: Any diagonal brace or girt with a bow or sweep exceeding L / 500 or exceeding 6.4 mm (1/4 in.) is REJECTED. Minor straightening is permitted only under an approved written engineering procedure by a cold-jacking method; heating members with an open flame (torch straightening) is strictly prohibited on quenched and tempered or high-strength low-alloy steels without metallurgical approval.

CLAUSE: API RP 4G Clause 8.3 — Maximum Allowable Corrosion Wall Thickness Loss for Primary Load Members
EXPLANATION: Corrosion (الصدأ وتآكل الجدار) significantly degrades the structural cross-sectional area and moment of inertia of hollow structural sections (HSS) and structural steel angles used in mast legs, substructure cellar deck beams, and crown water-table beams. Ultrasonic thickness (UT) grid mapping must be performed on all corrosion-prone areas, particularly lower mast bottom panels, mud-spray zones, and enclosed tubulars.
ACCEPTANCE: Maximum allowable uniform metal loss (wall thickness reduction) on primary load-bearing members (mast legs, substructure main load girders) shall not exceed 10% of the nominal original drawing thickness ($t_{act} \ge 0.90 \times t_{nom}$).
REJECTION: Any primary load-bearing member exhibiting remaining wall thickness less than 90% of nominal (metal loss > 10%) is strictly REJECTED. For secondary framing members (ladders, walkways, intermediate girts), wall loss exceeding 15% is REJECTED.

CLAUSE: API RP 4G Clause 6.1 — Category I Daily Inspection Requirements
EXPLANATION: Category I inspection (الفحص اليومي - الفئة الأولى) is an ongoing visual observation conducted while the structure is in service by the rig operating crew (driller, derrickman, toolpusher). It focuses on identifying obvious operational damage, unusual vibrations, foundation settling, loose fasteners, and damaged safety devices during daily drilling or workover operations.
ACCEPTANCE: Structure is acceptable if all safety pins are installed with keepers/cotter pins, wireline turnbuckles are safety-wired, no visible cracked welds or distorted braces are observed, and no unusual structural creaking or excessive lateral mast sway occurs during hoisting.
REJECTION: Presence of missing pin safety retainers, loose leveling shims, cracked weld indications visible to the naked eye, unanchored mast base, or foundation wash-out requires immediate cessation of hoisting until restored to compliant status.

CLAUSE: API RP 4G Clause 6.2 — Category II Weekly / Rig-Up Inspection Requirements
EXPLANATION: Category II inspection (فحص أسبوعي أو أثناء الفك والتركيب - الفئة الثانية) is a comprehensive visual walk-around inspection performed weekly or prior to raising/lowering the mast during rig moving operations. Performed by the rig toolpusher, drilling superintendent, or rig mechanic.
ACCEPTANCE: Acceptable when all mast raising lines (sheaves and wire rope) show zero broken wires or kinking; mast locking pins and hydraulic scoping cylinder locks engage completely; substructure spreaders and leveling jacks are within 3.2 mm (1/8 in.) level tolerance; and all ladder safety climbs and monkey board latching pins are fully functional.
REJECTION: Rejection occurs if mast raising wire ropes exhibit corrosion, kinks, or broken wires exceeding API RP 9B criteria; if hydraulic scoping cylinders show fluid bypass or failure to mechanically lock; or if mast shoes fail to seat fully on substructure pedestals.

CLAUSE: API RP 4G Clause 6.3 — Category III Periodic Thorough Inspection (2-Year / 730 Operating Days)
EXPLANATION: Category III inspection (الفحص الدوري الشامل - الفئة الثالثة) is a rigorous visual and non-destructive examination conducted at least once every two years (730 operating days) or prior to commencing work on a high-risk/deep-gas well. It must be performed by a designated qualified inspector possessing documented training in drilling structure failure modes, AWS welding inspection, and primary load path mechanics.
ACCEPTANCE: Requires 100% visual examination of all structural members, mast raising sheaves, crown sheaves, racking board fingers, and substructure load cells; Ultrasonic Thickness (UT) testing on suspect corrosion zones; and Magnetic Particle Inspection (MPI) or Liquid Penetrant (PT) on at least 10% of critical high-stress primary structural welds.
REJECTION: Any cracked structural weld, permanent member sweep exceeding L / 1000, pin hole elongation exceeding 1.6 mm (1/16 in.), or primary leg wall thinning exceeding 10% is strictly REJECTED. The structure cannot be cleared for operations until repaired and recertified by a written engineering report.

CLAUSE: API RP 4G Clause 6.4 — Category IV Major Overhaul, Teardown & Recertification (10-Year Baseline / 5-Year Offshore)
EXPLANATION: Category IV inspection (الفحص الشامل وإعادة الاعتماد - الفئة الرابعة) is a complete structural overhaul and re-certification required every 10 years for land rigs (or every 5 years for offshore drilling units or aggressive corrosive environments). It requires the mast to be lowered, completely disassembled, blast-cleaned to bare metal in critical joints, and inspected under the direct supervision of a Registered Professional Engineer (PE) or OEM Technical Representative.
ACCEPTANCE: Full 100% Non-Destructive Examination (WFMPI or Ultrasonic Testing per AWS D1.1 and ASNT Level II) on all critical primary load-bearing welds, mast shoes, pivot pins, crown block frame, scoping locks, and substructure pin lugs. Dimensional inspection of all pin connection bores with zero cracks and ovality $\le 1.6\text{ mm}$ (1/16 in.). A formal Category IV Certificate signed by a PE or OEM is issued.
REJECTION: Any fatigue crack in parent metal or primary structural welds, unapproved structural field alterations (unauthorized burn holes, uncertified welded brackets), or wall loss exceeding 10% that cannot be repaired per OEM welding procedure specification (WPS) results in permanent condemnation or formal derating of the structure's Maximum Static Hook Load.

CLAUSE: API RP 4G Clause 9.1 — Mast Pin Connection and Pin Hole Ovality Wear Limits
EXPLANATION: Masts and telescoping structures rely on heavy high-strength alloy steel pins (مسامير التثبيت ومحاور الوصل) to connect mast sections, A-frame legs, raising links, and substructure girders. Cyclic hoisting loads cause fretting wear, galling, and ovality elongation of the pin holes (ثقوب المسامير), leading to structural looseness, eccentric loading, and mast misalignment.
ACCEPTANCE: Pin holes must be circular within OEM drawing tolerances. The maximum allowable radial clearance between the pin OD and the bushing/pin hole ID shall not exceed 1.6 mm (1/16 in.) for pins up to 76 mm (3.0 in.) diameter, or 2.4 mm (3/32 in.) for larger pins, unless otherwise specified by the OEM.
REJECTION: Pin hole elongation, egg-shaping (ovality), or wall wear exceeding 1.6 mm (1/16 in.) over nominal pin size is REJECTED. Pinhole must be line-bored, fitted with OEM-approved hardened replacement bushings, or built up with qualified welding and re-machined per OEM engineering approval.

CLAUSE: API Spec 4F Section 7 / API RP 4G Clause 5.2 — Substructure Leveling and Foundation Elevation Tolerances
EXPLANATION: An unleveled substructure (قاعدة الحفارة) induces severe torsional twisting throughout the derrick and concentrates the hook load onto two legs rather than distributing it equally across all four mast shoes. Elevation difference between mast base support shoes must be verified using an optical surveyor's transit or precision laser level before raising the mast.
ACCEPTANCE: The maximum allowable elevation variation across the four mast shoe bearing surfaces or between substructure rotary support beams shall not exceed 3.2 mm (1/8 in.).
REJECTION: Elevation difference greater than 3.2 mm (1/8 in.) across mast shoes or substructure corners is REJECTED. The rig must be re-shimmed with solid, full-bearing steel shims; wooden cribbing or unanchored hollow spacers under mast shoes are strictly prohibited.

CLAUSE: API Spec 4F Section 8 / API RP 4G Clause 10 — Nameplate Data and Static Hook Load Derating
EXPLANATION: Every drilling structure must carry an authentic, permanent API Spec 4F Nameplate (لوحة بيانات الصاري) securely affixed to the structure. The nameplate defines the Maximum Rated Static Hook Load (أقصى حمل خطاف ساكن) for a specified number of lines strung to the traveling block, the maximum pipe setback capacity, and design wind ratings with and without setback.
ACCEPTANCE: Nameplate must be legible, stamped with manufacturer name, serial number, API Spec 4F monogram (if licensed), rated hook load capacity (e.g. 500,000 lbs, 750,000 lbs, 1,000,000 lbs), wind rating with setback (e.g. 80 knots), and raising line safety factor.
REJECTION: Operating a drilling structure without an authentic nameplate or missing load capacity documentation is strictly prohibited. If structural repairs, damaged unreplaced members, or severe generalized corrosion occur, a Professional Engineer or the OEM must formally de-rate the hook load capacity and issue a revised engineering nameplate.

CLAUSE: API RP 4G Appendix A — Mast Guyline Sizing, Anchor Pull Testing, and Pretension Limits
EXPLANATION: For guyed masts (الصواري المثبتة بالحبال السلكية), guylines prevent wind overturning and counteract racking forces during heavy pulling operations. Guylines must be rigged to engineered ground anchors (مرابط التثبيت الأرضية) positioned at the correct radius and angle specified in the mast rigging diagram.
ACCEPTANCE: Mast guylines must be minimum 14.5 mm (9/16 in.) or 16 mm (5/8 in.) 6x19 / 6x36 IWRC Extra Improved Plow Steel (EIPS) wire rope. Guyline pretension must be adjusted to between 4.45 kN and 13.3 kN (1,000 to 3,000 lbs) as measured with a calibrated tensiometer. Ground anchors must have a verified pull-test holding capacity of at least 9,000 kg (20,000 lbs) or 1.5 times the maximum calculated guyline load, re-tested at intervals not exceeding 24 months.
REJECTION: Any guyline exhibiting corrosion, flattened strands, or more than 3 broken wires in one strand within one lay length is REJECTED. Ground anchors showing soil pull-out, cracking in concrete deadmen, or failure to hold the 20,000 lbs pull test are condemned immediately.

CLAUSE: API RP 4G Clause 4.2 / AWS D1.1 — Welding Repair and NDT Personnel Qualifications
EXPLANATION: Field welding repairs on drilling structures involve high-strength structural steels (such as ASTM A572 Grade 50, A514, or structural HSS tubing) that are highly susceptible to hydrogen-induced cracking and heat-affected zone (HAZ) embrittlement if welded without preheat or proper consumable control.
ACCEPTANCE: All welding repairs on mast and substructure load-bearing members must be executed by certified welders qualified in accordance with AWS D1.1 (Structural Welding Code - Steel) using pre-qualified or qualified Welding Procedure Specifications (WPS) with low-hydrogen electrodes (E7018 / E8018) and monitored preheat (minimum 100°C / 212°F on thick alloy plates). NDT inspections (MPI / UT) must be performed by ASNT SNT-TC-1A / ISO 9712 Level II certified technicians.
REJECTION: Welding performed by uncertified welders, absence of a written approved WPS, welding in rainy/high-wind conditions without wind shielding, or presence of arc strikes on primary structural steel is strictly REJECTED. Arc strikes must be ground smooth and inspected with MT.
