[STANDARD: ASME IX]
[TITLE: ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code Section IX - Welding, Brazing, and Fusing Qualifications]
[ARTICLE: QW-200 — Welding Procedure Qualifications]

[CLAUSE: QW-200.1 — Welding Procedure Specification (WPS)]
A Welding Procedure Specification (WPS) is a written qualified welding procedure prepared to provide direction for making production welds to Code requirements. The WPS shall reference the supporting Procedure Qualification Record (PQR). The WPS shall describe all essential, nonessential, and when required, supplementary essential variables for each welding process used.
Required WPS content: welding process, base metal P-number, filler metal F-number and A-number, positions, preheat temperature, PWHT, shielding gas, electrical characteristics, technique. The WPS shall be available to the welder during production welding.
ACCEPTANCE: WPS completed on Form QW-482, signed and dated, references supporting PQR(s), available at work site.
REJECTION: WPS with blank essential variable fields. WPS not referencing a qualified PQR. WPS not available to welder.
---

[CLAUSE: QW-200.2 — Procedure Qualification Record (PQR)]
A Procedure Qualification Record (PQR) is a record of welding variables used to produce an acceptable test weldment and the results of tests conducted on the weldment to qualify a WPS. The PQR shall be a complete and accurate record of the actual conditions used during welding of the test coupon.
Required PQR content: actual values of all essential variables used, nondestructive test results, mechanical test results, and certification that the coupon was welded and tested in accordance with ASME IX requirements. The organization performing the qualification shall certify and retain the PQR.
ACCEPTANCE: PQR completed on Form QW-483, all actual values recorded, mechanical tests all pass, signed and certified by responsible organization.
REJECTION: PQR with estimated or planned (not actual) values. Missing mechanical test results. Unsigned or uncertified PQR.
---

[CLAUSE: QW-201 — Manufacturer's or Contractor's Responsibility]
Each manufacturer or contractor is responsible for the welding done by their organization and shall conduct the tests required by ASME IX to qualify the welding procedures and the performance of welders and welding operators used in construction. A manufacturer or contractor may use a WPS qualified by another manufacturer or contractor provided the first manufacturer/contractor certifies the PQR. A manufacturer may not use a welder qualified by another contractor without re-qualification — welder qualifications are NOT transferable.
ACCEPTANCE: Manufacturer/contractor qualified their own WPS or has documented transfer of PQR from qualifying organization.
REJECTION: Using another organization's WPS without documented PQR certification. Accepting another company's welder qualification without re-qualification.
---

[CLAUSE: QW-251 — Essential Variables for SMAW (Covered Electrode)]
The following are essential variables for SMAW — a change in any requires re-qualification:
- Change in P-number of base metal (e.g., P-1 carbon steel to P-3 alloy steel)
- Change in F-number of filler metal (F-1, F-2, F-3, F-4, F-5, F-6)
- Change in A-number (chemical composition of deposited weld metal)
- Increase in base metal thickness beyond qualified range (T to 2T for T≥12mm)
- Change in position (if impact testing required — add position)
- Change from PWHT to no PWHT or vice versa
- Change from preheat of 100°F or above to below 100°F
- Addition or deletion of backing
ACCEPTANCE: WPS remains within all essential variable limits of supporting PQR.
REJECTION: Any change in essential variable without requalification. Operating outside the ranges qualified by the PQR.
---

[CLAUSE: QW-301.1 — Welder Performance Qualification — General]
Each welder shall be qualified by making a test weld under the supervision of the manufacturer or contractor. The test weld shall be examined and tested as required. A welder who successfully completes the mechanical or visual tests required is qualified for the variables used during the test. Welder performance qualifications shall be documented on Form QW-484.
ACCEPTANCE: Welder qualification documented on QW-484, signed and certified, showing all variables and test results.
REJECTION: Welder working without a valid performance qualification record. QW-484 with missing information.
---

[CLAUSE: QW-304 — Welder Performance Qualification Test — Pipe]
For pipe welding qualification:
- Test position 1G (flat, pipe horizontal, roll) qualifies: flat position only.
- Test position 2G (vertical, pipe vertical) qualifies: flat and horizontal.
- Test position 5G (horizontal, pipe horizontal, fixed) qualifies: flat, vertical, and overhead.
- Test position 6G (inclined, pipe at 45°, fixed) qualifies: all positions (flat, horizontal, vertical, overhead). THIS IS THE MOST COMMON OIL & GAS PIPE WELDER QUALIFICATION.
Pipe diameter tested qualifies from 73mm (2-7/8 in) to unlimited if test pipe ≥73mm OD.
ACCEPTANCE: Welder qualified for all positions welded in production, in the base metal P-number group, and wall thickness range covered.
REJECTION: Welder performing work in a position not covered by their qualification. Wrong P-number or wall thickness.
---

[CLAUSE: QW-321 — Continuity of Welder Qualifications]
A welder's qualification remains in effect indefinitely unless the welder has not welded with the process for a period of six months or more, or there is specific reason to question the ability of the welder. If a welder has not welded with a specific process for 6 months, their qualification for that process expires and they must re-qualify.
ACCEPTANCE: Welder has welded with the process within the past 6 months as documented in continuity records.
REJECTION: Welder qualification expired (>6 months no welding with that process) without re-qualification. No continuity records maintained.
---

[CLAUSE: QW-322 — Renewal of Welder Qualifications]
An expired welder qualification may be renewed by welding a single test coupon in any one of the positions for which the welder was previously qualified. The test coupon shall be examined by radiographic examination or bend tests. If the results are acceptable, the qualification is renewed.
ACCEPTANCE: Renewal coupon passes RT or bend tests.
REJECTION: Renewal attempted without formal test. Renewal by production weld RT alone without procedure weld.
---

[CLAUSE: QW-451 — Procedure Qualification — Base Metal Thickness Limits]
The following thickness ranges are qualified by procedure test coupons:

TEST COUPON THICKNESS (T) | QUALIFIED THICKNESS RANGE:
- T < 1.5 mm (1/16 in): T minimum to 2T maximum
- 1.5 mm ≤ T < 10 mm: 1.5 mm minimum to 2T maximum
- T = 10 mm to 19 mm: 5 mm minimum to 2T maximum
- T ≥ 19 mm: 5 mm minimum to 2T maximum — no maximum limit
- For impact testing required: see specific table for narrower ranges

For weld metal deposited: maximum qualified weld metal thickness = 2× the thickness deposited in the test coupon (or unlimited for welds deposited in multiple passes in a single layer).
ACCEPTANCE: Production weld base metal thickness and weld metal thickness within qualified ranges.
REJECTION: Welding base metal or depositing weld metal outside qualified thickness range.
---

[CLAUSE: QW-461 — Position Limitations]
Positions for groove welds:
1G — Flat (plate horizontal, welding on top surface). Qualifies: 1G only.
2G — Horizontal (plate vertical, weld axis horizontal). Qualifies: 1G, 2G.
3G — Vertical (plate vertical, weld axis vertical). Qualifies: 1G, 3G, and with 4G: all positions.
4G — Overhead (plate horizontal, welding from below). Qualifies: 1G, 4G.
3G + 4G together — Qualifies: all positions.
6G — Pipe inclined 45° fixed. Qualifies: all positions for pipe.
ACCEPTANCE: Production welds performed only in positions qualified by performance test.
REJECTION: Welder performing overhead welding when only qualified 1G flat. Welder performing 5G pipe when only qualified 6G is acceptable — note 6G covers all, but 5G does NOT qualify 2G.
---

[CLAUSE: QW-482 — Suggested Format for WPS]
Mandatory minimum information on a WPS:
- WPS Number and Revision
- Date and Supporting PQR Number(s)
- Welding Process and Type (manual/semi-auto/auto)
- Base Metals — P-Number, Group, Grade
- Filler Metals — SFA Spec, AWS Classification, F-Number, A-Number
- Positions qualified
- Preheat: minimum preheat and maximum interpass temperature
- PWHT: temperature range and time
- Shielding gas type and flow rate
- Joint design sketch or reference
- Current type (DCEP/DCEN/AC), polarity, amperage and voltage ranges
- Travel speed range
- Technique: stringer or weave, pass sequence
ACCEPTANCE: WPS contains all listed items with ranges.
REJECTION: WPS missing any mandatory field. Ranges not specified (only nominal values). No joint design reference.
